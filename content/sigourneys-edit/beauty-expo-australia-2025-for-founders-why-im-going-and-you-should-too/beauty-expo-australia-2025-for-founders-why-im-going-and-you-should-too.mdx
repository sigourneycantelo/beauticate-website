Beauty Expo Australia isn't just a trade show. It's the heartbeat of Australia's professional beauty scene. As someone who's covered this industry for over two decades, I can tell you firsthand: this is where the magic happens. It's where I've discovered incredible brands, had some of the most inspiring founder conversations, and walked away with enough beauty intel to last the year.

This August I'm heading back to Beauty Expo Australia at ICC Sydney, and I'd love for you to come too. Tickets are moving fast, especially with the current price drop ending soon, so grab yours before it closes [here](https://www.beautyexpoaustralia.com.au/).

**Use the code BEAUTICATE20 at checkout for 20% off your tickets.**

## What Makes It So Special?

Whether you're a salon owner, a brand founder, an RN, or just someone who lives and breathes beauty, Beauty Expo is a game changer.

It's not just the access to new products, though the aisles are packed with incredible innovations. It's the energy, the people, the conversations. You'll leave feeling inspired, connected, and ready to take your business or career to the next level.

> "Some of my favourite beauty discoveries have happened at Beauty Expo - it's where you really see the next wave before it hits mainstream."

## What I'm Excited About This Year

The lineup is shaping up to be amazing. I'll be keeping a close eye on the Aesthetic Clinic and Injectables spaces, both flagged as major growth categories this year, alongside the emerging brands coming through the Beauty Discovery Zone.

It's such a rare chance to explore what's emerging in skincare, devices, injectables and professional treatments, all in one place. I love seeing how brands present themselves, tell their stories, and connect with their community.

Plus, the education sessions are always a highlight for me. This year's conference streams - Art of Aesthetics, The Business of Beauty, and Dermal Health & Wellness - mean you can filter straight to what's relevant to you, whether that's technical skill-building, business growth, or the neurowellness and holistic health sessions that are having a real moment.

Hearing directly from founders and industry experts about how they've built their businesses, and survived the tough stuff, is both inspiring and incredibly real.

## Come Be Part of the Story

This year, [Beauticate](/) is back on the Beauty Expo floor. I'll be catching up with some of the industry's most inspiring names, and we'll be capturing behind-the-scenes content along the way.

If you're there, come say hi. And if you haven't got your ticket yet, use the code below for 20% off before prices go up: **BEAUTICATE20**

## Let's Do This

There's something about being in a space filled with beauty pros that's hard to explain - it's electric.

So whether you're deep in the industry or new to it, come along either way. Let's meet, get inspired, and celebrate everything this amazing community has to offer.

P.S. Beauty Expo is a trade-only event, so you'll need to be a qualified professional to attend.
