Because I love you and I want you to REALLY enjoy your experience on [Beauticate Shop](/shop), I've managed to organise some special gifts for my lovely customers. Beauticate Shop is launching a rotating gift with purchase, one brand at a time, so we can introduce each properly. Be among the first to shop that brand while it's live, for the minimum spend, and the gift lands in your order automatically. ...

<PullQuote>No code, no fine print, nothing to scroll down and find the catch on.</PullQuote>

... It just appears, shipped straight to you from the brand itself.

Serums, hair masks, fragrance, a luminiser I use EVERY day, so much loot, you might develop a little Beauticate Shop habit. And that's exactly the point. Next up will be our loyalty program! But more on that later.

We're calling it the gifting suite, and it kicks off this month with BOOIE Beauty.

## BOOIE Beauty, first through the door

[Celeste Barber](/interviews/actors-presenters/celeste-barber-on-adhd-bullying-boundaries-and-the-battle-with-social-media) built [BOOIE](/shop/brands/booie-beauty) on a simple complaint, that most makeup aimed at women her age is too complicated and sometimes downright preachy. So she launched a tight edit of easy-to-use essentials that I've completely fallen in love with. Buy from the range this month and a full-size Bloody Delicious illuminator, the one she and I both can't go without, lands in your cart free.

## Saint Louve, straight after

Melbourne formulator Elly Seymour spent fifteen years in the beauty industry before building [Saint Louve](/shop/brands/saint-louve) around a background in biology and chemistry rather than marketing. The serums are trialled on real participants over 28 days, tracked again at three and six months, and packaged in airless glass so the actives stay stable. Her gift is a full-size Vitamin B3, B5 and Hyaluronic Acid serum, the one that's already picked up a Best Daily Essential Radiance Serum award.

<CollectionRail handle="saint-louve" title="Shop Saint Louve" />

## St. Louis Says brings the hair mask

Two Brisbane barbers, Adrian Mezzina and Tim Persley, spent years behind the chair at their own salon before deciding the products they were recommending daily should exist under their own name. Everything's made at their own Sunshine Coast facility now, formulated with amaranth extract and hydrolysed rice protein for strands that need real strength back. [Their gift](/shop/brands/st-louis-says) is the Resurrection Repair Treatment Mask.

<CollectionRail handle="st-louis-says" title="Shop St. Louis Says" />

## BonWellness, worn rather than swallowed

Nicole O'Neil built [BonWellness](/shop/brands/bonwellness) around a simple bet, that the actives in a supplement don't need to be swallowed to work. Her wearable patches deliver them through the skin instead. Her gift is an assortment of her patches for anyone who'd rather not add another capsule to the pile on the bathroom sink.

<CollectionRail handle="bonwellness" title="Shop BonWellness" />

## Estetika, for the everyday carry

A Burgundy MINEE cardholder with a gold chain, small enough to actually use, sturdy enough to survive being thrown into every bag you own. I'm obsessed and I love it so much I sometimes use it as a handbag! [Estetika](/shop/brands/estetika) is the brand behind it.

<CollectionRail handle="estetika" title="Shop Estetika" />

## Subtle Energies, an old favourite

One of the longest-running names in Australian aromatherapy, still making the kind of blends that smell like they were formulated by someone who actually understands essential oils rather than someone chasing a trend. [Subtle Energies](/shop/brands/subtle-energies) blend oils for the top spas in the world and my favourite all time facial oil is their facial oil blend. The gift is a full-size Aura Protection Body Mist. I mist this before I meditate and I swear it changes my state.

<CollectionRail handle="subtle-energies" title="Shop Subtle Energies" />

## buj, timed for a reason

[Buj](/shop/brands/buj) is the most luxe cream just for your breasts and it lands during Breast Cancer Awareness Month, deliberately. The brand builds self-care and body products designed to fit into a life that's already full, which felt like the right week to spotlight it.

<CollectionRail handle="buj" title="Shop buj" />

## Lash Armour, saved for party season

My favourite [magnetic lashes](/shop/brands/lash-armour) that make putting on a fluttery pair SO easy you won't believe it. This will drop around party season on purpose. The founder Michelle is donating a lash and liner combo that you will quickly fall in love with.

<CollectionRail handle="lash-armour" title="Shop Lash Armour" />

## Tulita, a fragrance tasting menu

[Tulita](/shop/brands/tulita) blends ancient Ayurvedic chakra balancing with modern perfumery, refillable bottles and oh my gosh have you seen more beautiful bottles. I'm obsessed with the precious lids.

<CollectionRail handle="tulita" title="Shop Tulita" />

## Sunescape, my actual go-to tan

I'll say it outright, this isn't just my favourite gift on this list, it's one of my favourite [tans](/beauty-style/beauty-tips/video-my-tanning-routine-sigourneys-edit) full stop. I use it constantly, through every wedding, every summer event, the lot. No patchiness, no biscuit smell, no giving away that you didn't just get back from four days somewhere hot. [The gift](/shop/brands/sunescape) is a full-size Instant Self Tan Mousse, vegan and streak-free, for whenever the sun clocks off for the year.

<CollectionRail handle="sunescape" title="Shop Sunescape" />

That's the lineup through summer, though the order can shift a little as we go. What won't change is the idea behind it. Every brand gets a proper moment instead of a mention, and you get something free for shopping somewhere small instead of somewhere enormous.
