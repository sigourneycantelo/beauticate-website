  - question: "What is A-beauty skincare?"
    answer: "A-beauty refers to skincare formulated with Australian botanical ingredients, a category that's still in its infancy but growing rapidly. Brands like Minenssey are pioneering this space by harnessing the unique survival properties of Australian flora developed through extreme climates and vast landscapes. The movement positions Australia as a major player in the global skincare market."
  - question: "What are Minenssey Absolute serums?"
    answer: "Minenssey Absolutes are highly concentrated plant extract serums that contain 99% pure botanical ingredient with minimal fillers like water or glycerin. This new wave of formulation makes the plant the hero of the product, delivering potent skincare elixirs designed to make serious changes to the skin. Each Absolute is tailored to specific skincare benefits, from hydration to anti-ageing."
  - question: "What does Flannel Flower Absolute do for skin?"
    answer: "Flannel Flower Absolute is an intensely hydrating serum that can be applied post-cleanse before moisturiser. It delivers the slip and silkiness of a traditional serum with the hydration benefits of a facial oil, but without any greasy residue. Due to its potency, only two drops are recommended per application."
  - question: "What are the benefits of Strawberry Gum and Snake Vine Absolutes?"
    answer: "Strawberry Gum Absolute features antibacterial properties from Strawberry Gum Eucalyptus and offers protection against sun and environmental damage while deeply nourishing the skin. Snake Vine Absolute harnesses a bio-mimic extraction method to preserve pure plant nutrients and deliver powerful antioxidants that leave skin firm and taut, making it an effective anti-ageing step."
