There's a question I get asked constantly. In DMs, in WhatsApp chats, at dinner parties, at school pickup. Sometimes from strangers, sometimes from women I've known for twenty years. The question is always some version of the same thing: *just tell me what to buy.*

Not a list. Not a mood board. What would you actually *choose*. And for all of my twenty-six years writing about beauty, first at Vogue, then here, apparently the most useful thing I've ever done is be the person my friends text when they need to know where to buy a good candle.

So we built a shop. And then we rebuilt the entire website to house it. (More on that backstory soon, and it's a good one).

---

## What this place is

Beauticate has always been about trusted recommendations, not listings. Content and commerce working together, not bolted on. You read here, you discover here, and now you can buy here, with the same editorial eye running through all of it.

We are editors first. Every product in the shop has been tested, considered and chosen by me, the Beauticate Collective, or our guest curators. Nothing there is because a brand paid for placement. Everything is there because it has earned its spot.

<PullQuote>Nothing is here because a brand paid for placement. Everything is here because it has earned its spot.</PullQuote>

We also care deeply about *who* is behind the brands we stock. Many of them are female-founded, Australian-made, or both, and that's *not* a coincidence. Over time you'll hear directly from the founders themselves: through the podcast, guest edits, through events where we bring them together with the Beauticate community. It's one of the things I'm most excited about.

## Meet some of the founders behind the shop

This is what I love about the brands we've chosen. There are real people behind them with real stories.

[Lisa Walker](/wellness/health/what-nobody-told-me-about-my-body-after-40), co-founder of [Eir Women](/shop/collections/eir-women), wrote one of the most personal pieces we've published (about what nobody tells you about your body after 40). It's worth your time.

And if you want to understand why we chose the brands we chose, start with the podcast. I've sat down with [Jessica Sepel, founder of JS Health](/vodcast/episodes/jess-sepel-on-ocd-healing-from-disordered-eating-grief-and-building-jshealth-wit), [Mukti Organics](/vodcast/episodes/mukti-organics-founder-on-trauma-truth-and-building-a-beauty-brand-with-purpose), and [Celeste Barber on Boo Beauty](/vodcast/episodes/celeste-barber-on-adhd-bullying-boundaries-and-the-battle-with-social-media). Three very different founders, three very honest conversations.

The idea for the shop was always there. But it took a coffee almost a year ago with Nicole O'Neil, founder of [Bon Wellness](/shop/collections/bon-patch), to make it real. I'll tell that story properly soon. For now: Nicole, thank you.

## What's in the shop

**Fragrance:** [Lumira](/shop/collections/lumira-1) and [Tulita](/shop/collections/tulita-parfum), two of the most beautiful boutique fragrance brands in the country.

**Skincare:** Australian botanical skincare from [Mukti Organics](/shop/collections/mukti-organics-1), plus [SaintLouve](/shop/collections/saintlouve-1), [La Mav](/shop/collections/lamav) and [Subtle Energies](/shop/collections/subtle-energies-2).

**Wellness:** [JS Health](/shop/collections/jshealth-vitamins-aus) and [Eir Women](/shop/collections/eir-women).

**Lifestyle:** One of my all-time favourite brands, [Maison Balzac](/shop/collections/maison-balzac-1).

More brands are coming, including some seriously exciting skincare and wellness names working through onboarding right now. And soon: Style (wardrobe staples for real life), Living (great things for your home) and Travel (everything you need to pack to stay sane).

You can shop by category, by brand, or by moment: the [Deepest Sleep](/shop/collections/evening-unwind) edit, the [Winter Edit](/shop/collections/autumn-edit), [Self-Care Sunday](/shop/collections/selfcare-sunday). A shop that thinks the way I think.
