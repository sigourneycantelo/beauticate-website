<PullQuote>The question I get is always some version of the same thing: "just tell me what to buy."</PullQuote>

There's a question I get asked constantly. In DMs, in WhatsApp chats, at dinner parties, at school pickup. Sometimes from strangers, sometimes from women I've known for twenty years. The question is always some version of the same thing: *just tell me what to buy.*

They don't want a mood board or a list of forty things they'll screenshot and never look at again. They want to know what I would actually choose. The LED mask I'd actually buy. The candle that's actually worth it. The fragrance that's been sitting on my bathroom shelf for three years, not because I forgot about it, but because I keep reaching for it.

I've been writing about beauty and wellness for twenty-six years. My first beauty writing gig was at an indie magazine called *Your Destiny* when I was 18. From there, *TV Week*, where I had my own beauty and fashion page (and where I once had to rub bronzing cream on Chris Hemsworth's chest during a cover shoot because the makeup artist had gone home. Someone had to do it). Then *New Woman*, then *Glamour*, then *Vogue Australia* for the better part of a decade. Then Beauticate, which I founded in 2014 because I wanted to build something more personal than a magazine and more honest than the algorithm-driven beauty internet we were all starting to drown in. And for all of that time, the most useful thing I have apparently ever done is be the person my friends text when they need to know where to buy a good candle.

So we built a shop. Then we rebuilt the entire website. Let me show you what's new.

## What's new

### [01 — Beauticate Shop](/shop)

This is where it all started. An edit of beauty, wellness and lifestyle products I actually use and believe in, with every order fulfilled directly by the brand. Nothing is here because someone paid for placement. Everything is here because it has earned its spot.

The launch lineup includes fragrance from [Lumira](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/lumira-1) and [Tulita](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/tulita-parfum), Australian botanical skincare from [Mukti Organics](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/mukti-organics-1), wellness from [JS Health](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/jshealth-vitamins-aus) and [Eir Women](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/eir-women), skin from [SaintLouve](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/saintlouve-1), [La Mav](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/lamav) and [Subtle Energies](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/subtle-energies-2), and one of my all-time favourite lifestyle brands, [Maison Balzac](https://beauticate-website.vercel.app/shop/collections/maison-balzac-1). You can shop by category, by brand, or by moment: the [Deepest Sleep](https://beauticate.shop/collections/evening-unwind) edit, the [Winter Edit](https://beauticate.shop/collections/autumn-edit), Self-Care Sunday. A shop that thinks the way I think.

I've got some seriously exciting skincare and wellness brands coming — it's just working through the various levels of their business to get them onboarded that takes time. But I can't wait to share as they join. And then I'll be adding more brands to Style (think wardrobe staples for real life), plus Living (great things for your home) and Travel (everything you need to pack to stay sane).

## A note on what you might find broken

The new site is live. The shop is open. And if you spot something that looks wrong — a broken image, a scrambled article, a link that goes nowhere — please go easy on me. Six weeks of five AM starts got us a long way. It didn't get us all the way.

If you find something, I want to know. Email us at hello@beauticate.com and we'll fix it.

## What I need from you

Tell me what brands you think should be in the shop that aren't. Tell me what's missing from your routine that you wish I'd curate. Tell me what doesn't work. Your feedback is how we decide what to build next.

The shop has been nearly a year in the making, with a surprise six-week website rebuild tucked in at the end. There were moments I wanted to walk away from all of it.

But I kept coming back to that original question.

*Just tell me what to buy.*

Here's my answer. [Come and have a look.](https://beauticate.shop)

*The edit, so you don't have to.*
