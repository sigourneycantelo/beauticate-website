  - question: "How do you make almond milk at home?"
    answer: "Homemade almond milk is surprisingly simple — soak raw almonds overnight, blend them with fresh water, then strain through a nut milk bag or cheesecloth. The result is a creamy, preservative-free milk that tastes far better than most store-bought versions. You can adjust the thickness by varying the water-to-almond ratio."
  - question: "Is homemade almond milk healthier than store-bought?"
    answer: "Homemade almond milk avoids the added sugars, preservatives, and heavy dilution common in commercial varieties. Making it yourself preserves more of the naturally occurring calcium, iron, zinc, and vitamin E found in almonds. It is also better for your skin, as vitamin E is a powerful antioxidant that supports a healthy glow from the inside out."
  - question: "How long does homemade almond milk last in the fridge?"
    answer: "Fresh homemade almond milk typically lasts three to five days when stored in a sealed container in the refrigerator. Because it contains no preservatives, it has a shorter shelf life than commercial alternatives. Give it a good shake before each use, as natural separation is normal."

Almond milk has been on our ‘super’ grocery check-list for some time thanks to its bounty of health boosting benefits: it's packed with calcium, iron and zinc. And, not to mention, our favourite beauty ingredient, vitamin E – amazing for glowing skin, from the inside, out. Oh, and it’s also brain food. But, as with many store bought varieties of healthy foods and beverages, it’s hard to keep track of what is actually going into your body. Added sugar? Preservatives? Heavily diluted, eliminating potent nutrients? Well, the best way to get around this is to make your own – a healthy habit Beauticate founder, Sigourney, has come to love. Here, she shows you to make your own almond milk – it’s surprisingly easy and it’ll keep your (now nutrient fed) mind at ease.
