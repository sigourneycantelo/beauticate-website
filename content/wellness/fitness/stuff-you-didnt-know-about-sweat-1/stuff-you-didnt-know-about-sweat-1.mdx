  - question: "Why do some people sweat more than others?"
    answer: "Sweat production varies based on genetics, fitness level, body composition, and environmental factors. People who exercise regularly may actually sweat more efficiently as their bodies become better at regulating temperature. Hormonal changes and certain medical conditions can also influence how much you perspire."
  - question: "Is sweating good for your skin?"
    answer: "Sweating can help flush out toxins and unclog pores, which may improve skin clarity over time. However, leaving sweat on the skin for too long can lead to breakouts and irritation. It is important to cleanse the skin after a heavy sweat session to reap the benefits without the downsides."
  - question: "Does sweating help you lose weight?"
    answer: "Sweating itself does not burn fat — the weight lost through perspiration is primarily water, which is regained once you rehydrate. However, the exercise that causes sweating does burn calories and contribute to weight loss. Staying properly hydrated during workouts is essential for both performance and recovery."
