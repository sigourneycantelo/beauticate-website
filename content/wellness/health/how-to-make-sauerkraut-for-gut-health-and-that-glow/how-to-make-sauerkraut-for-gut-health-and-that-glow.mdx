  - question: "How do you make sauerkraut at home?"
    answer: "Homemade sauerkraut requires just cabbage and salt. Shred the cabbage, massage it with salt until it releases its juices, then pack it tightly into a mason jar. Keep it submerged under its own brine and leave it to ferment at room temperature for one to four weeks."
  - question: "Is sauerkraut good for gut health?"
    answer: "Yes, sauerkraut is one of the best natural sources of probiotics, the beneficial bacteria that support a healthy gut microbiome. Regular consumption can aid digestion, reduce bloating and contribute to clearer, more radiant skin thanks to the gut-skin connection."
  - question: "How long does homemade sauerkraut last?"
    answer: "Once fermented to your liking, homemade sauerkraut can be stored in the refrigerator for several months. The cold temperature slows fermentation while preserving the live probiotics, so it stays tangy and crunchy for a long time."
