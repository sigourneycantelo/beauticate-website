<QuickAnswer>

Rae Morris is one of Australia’s most celebrated makeup artists — a self-taught creative who went from a Brisbane salon to working with Naomi Campbell, designing her own magnetic brush range, and writing three bestselling beauty books. This 2013 interview captures Rae at home, unfiltered, on how a happy accident with a lip gloss changed everything.

</QuickAnswer>

*Rae Morris is an Australian makeup legend who brims over with a contagious passion for what she does. She has penned three makeup books, was spokesperson for L’Oreal for ten years and launched her eponymous range of makeup brushes to sell out success. All this from someone who didn’t want to be a makeup artist… We caught up with the gorgeous Rae in her home and drooled over her incredible makeup kit.*

This is our original interview with Rae Morris, first published in December 2013. We have since sat down with Rae for an updated conversation covering her life today, her evolved beauty philosophy, and the challenges she has navigated since.

## Rae's Unlikely Start in Makeup

<StickyScroll src="/content/interviews/creatives/rae-morris/rae-morris-p-4-1.jpg" alt="Rae Morris sitting on stairs with her dog Duffer, a Maltese cross" side="right" caption="Rae with her dog, Duffer, a Maltese cross">

<PullQuote>I Never Thought I Was That Good at Makeup.</PullQuote>

I was a complete tomboy. We lived on a farm with goats and ducks and horses. I thought I wanted to be a vet because I always played with animals and dolls that had lots of fluffy hair. Every doll mum bought me I would shave its head, or cut or bleach its hair. I gave one doll a flat top. Hairdressing was what I fell into first. I started hairdressing at 14. Mum was in the fitness industry, competing as a body builder. I used to come home when I was little and sit on her back when she did push ups because she needed extra weight! My dad was a builder. How I ended up in this industry I have no idea!

I loved hairdressing but I had bad carpal tunnels in my wrist and I couldn't hold a hairdryer so I managed the salon and started helping hairdressers and photographers on shoots. I would help with hair and then hang out in the makeup area and I thought I would love to do this, I'm not very good at it but I'll give it a go.

## Rae's Magnetic Brush Innovation

<StickyScroll src="/content/interviews/creatives/rae-morris/rae-morris-p-7-1.jpg" alt="Rae Morris magnetic makeup brushes standing upright" side="left" caption="Rae's new makeup brushes have magnets in their base so they can stand up by themselves">

<PullQuote>The Real Twist of Fate Was Naomi Campbell...</PullQuote>

I was doing hair for a modelling pageant in Istanbul and she was one of the judges. She had a makeup artist there, of course, but they had stormed out. Naomi asked for lip gloss and all I did was put lip gloss on her lips. It was two seconds of her life, she'll never remember, but it changed mine because I got a snapshot of me putting lip gloss on Naomi Campbell and I was now this International makeup artist and I came back to Brisbane and I was in the press and doing interviews and I sort of lied myself through it and taught myself on the job.

<PullQuote>I was 20. I lived with a drag queen back in Brisbane and he would teach me this and that.</PullQuote>

Then I ended up training with Richard Sharah, he was David Bowie's makeup artist. He said, 'Right, you're moving to Sydney!' and I assisted him, then I went on to do fashion week and magazine jobs and L'Oreal after that.

Every time I used to buy makeup brushes I would soften them with bleach and shave with a razor them into a fine point because I never loved the shape. A brush company approached me and said, 'Do you want to do some brushes?' I went, 'No, I don't, but I would like you to make them specifically for me.'

</StickyScroll>

<div className="article-wide not-prose my-8">

<PullQuote>I Never Wanted to Have a Brand or Be That Person.</PullQuote>

I was too busy doing makeup. When I started to get those brushes made, it was honestly demand. Every time I touched a models face, I remember Victoria Baron picking one up and I remember Jennifer Hawkins when I used the big radiance brush she said, 'I want this where can I buy one?' and the rest is history. We've got a new magnetic thing and you pick up fifty brushes and you put them on a table and they stand up instantly. To be honest they were only made for me, the fact that they sell... I'm not about sales. But for me, personally, I can't do makeup without them. If they sell, that's a bonus. I mean that, and the response! I've had Dita Von Teese want them... [Pink](http://www.pinkspage.com/au), Kelly Rowland, Sarah Jessica Parker order them. It's been amazing.

## Rae's Skincare for Acne-Prone Skin

<StickyScroll src="/content/interviews/creatives/rae-morris/rae-morris-p-1-1.jpg" alt="Skincare essentials: Cetaphil cleanser, Bioderma Crealine, Clarisonic and L'Oreal Nude Magique" side="right" caption="Skincare essentials: Cetaphil cleanser, Bioderma Crealine makeup remover, the Clarisonic and L'Oreal Nude Magique">

<PullQuote>I Break Out Really Badly from Makeup.</PullQuote>

My skin goes crap. I use [Clinique](https://www.clinique.com.au/) Even Better Skin. I've also fallen in love with [DMK](https://dannemking.com/) Cream based foundation, it's medical, it's really sheer, you can't see it but it stops bacteria on contact and calms acne down. I use it as a concealer. I put on one or two drops [Giorgio Armani](https://www.giorgioarmanibeauty.com.au/) Number 2 Luminiser, it's great for pale skin. Sometimes I'll use [L'Oreal](https://www.lorealparis.com.au/) Nude Magique as an alternative to foundation, I mix it up. Also [Laura Mercier](https://www.lauramercier.com/) Secret Camouflage concealer.

I use a cream blush by Stila or NARS. I love [Lancôme](https://www.lancome.com.au/) Juicy Tube Lipgloss, it's a taste thing. And I love L'Oreal Telescopic Mascara. [YSL](https://www.yslbeauty.com.au/) Lipstick Rouge Pur Couture. I use a smudgy kohl pencil, I love the NARS or [Bobbi Brown](https://www.bobbibrown.com.au/) gel pencils, you've got time to smudge them and then they don't move. I also like [Dior](https://www.dior.com/en_au/beauty) Diorshow mascara but the wand is too big so I use my little metal comb to apply it to get it right into the lashes.

For skin I use [Cetaphil](https://www.cetaphil.com.au/) cleanser, a bit of Crealine. I use the Clarisonic once a week. I love [Ultraceuticals](https://www.ultraceuticals.com/) as well. I love their brightening serum, it's one of my favourite things.

</StickyScroll>

<PullQuote>DMK Has Cleaned My Skin Like Nothing Ever Before.</PullQuote>

The deep pore cleanser and the spot cream is the best I've ever used in my life. I have this powdered Vitamin C mask in sachets, it's like having an omnilux light, the next day I've never seen my skin look that different. Because I'm trying to get natural, I rarely put anything on my body. I'm so white you never see my legs or arms anyway. Sometimes I use QV and I do love putting the [Goe Oil](https://www.mecca.com.au/) on elbows or knees.

## Rae's Undone Hair Philosophy

<PullQuote>I Like My Hair Undone.</PullQuote>

If I blowdry it I look like a news reporter. I have to wash it every day so I go to bed with it wet. If I wash it in the morning I use a couple of pumps of [Kerastase](https://www.kerastase.com.au/) oil, the gold [Redken](https://www.redken.com.au/) one or [Moroccanoil](https://www.moroccanoil.com/au_en). My hair is the driest hair in the world. I don't blowdry, tong or straighten it, it matts so badly. I hated my hair growing up. I wanted Farrah Fawcett flicks or dead straight hair. Even during my late twenties I used to straighten the hell out of it. It wasn't until when I was working heavily in the fashion industry and I started to see models would come in with hair like that and I thought you know, 'I'm just going to look how I look,' and then people would comment about my hair and I thought, 'Okay I'm going to keep with it.'

<PullQuote>Every time I touched a models face, they said, 'I want this, where can I buy one?'</PullQuote>

<PullQuote>Growing Up, I Hated Being So Fair.</PullQuote>

I was the one that was fully dressed, hat on. When I got burnt it blistered. It wasn't fun. Even now when people say, 'Let's go to the beach', I can not do 'beach'. Five minutes and I'm red. When I'm on a shoot, I will put sunscreen on every two hours religiously. I use [Invisible Zinc](https://www.invisiblezinc.com.au/). Even the [Bioderma](https://www.bioderma.com.au/) 50+, anything with zinc. And I wear hats, long sleeve shirts, the collar, long loose pants.

## Rae's Androgynous Style

<StickyScroll src="/content/interviews/creatives/rae-morris/rae-morris-p-6-1.jpg" alt="Rae Morris wearing a black Alexander McQueen dress" side="left" caption="Rae wears dress by Alexander McQueen">

<PullQuote>My Style Is Minimal and Androgynous.</PullQuote>

I don't want to be too girly, but I do like to wear black. I like men's fragrance. I wear [Tom Ford](https://www.tomford.com/) Black Orchid fragrance and [Francis Kurkdjian](https://www.franciskurkdjian.com/) Lumiere Noire, that's my favourite all the time. Gok Wan gave me this [Mad et Len](https://www.mad-et-len.com/) candle, it smells amazing.

I love not knowing what I'll do tomorrow. Every day's a different place, if you don't like someone you don't have to ever work with them again. I also love that when I go to work every single other person in that room is the best at what they do. It's a seamstress, or an actor or a photographer or a hairdresser. I'm around extraordinary talent every day of my life. I failed art at school. I had no patience. I wasn't methodical, I used to shortcut everything.

<PullQuote>I'm Dyslexic and I Write Books...</PullQuote>

And when I do makeup seminars I say, if I can do this, anyone can do this. No arts degree, failed school, dyslexic, whatever, can't even draw. And here I am! I've done three books, about to do my fourth in January. I love it.

</StickyScroll>
