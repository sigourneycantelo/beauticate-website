It’s not every day you get three models - who also happen to be friends - in a room together - as they swap beauty secrets and prep for a party. Sigourney got to hang out with Bambi Norwood-Blyth, Erin Heatherton and Zippora Seven as they got ready for the launch of the new Impulse Fragrances, for which they are the new faces. She soaked up a little beauty intel and managed to pick up a few clever tips along the way.
