#### As a busy mum and business owner, getting a full face done before I drop the kids at the school gate and work isn't always possible... So, I improvise. Welcome to our latest addition to Beauticate, Beauty Bites. Here I'll be filming easy, quick, on-the-go makeup tutorials in all of my favourite cafes, restaurants and bars that invariably end up being my segues between home, work, catch-ups and meetings. Today, I go the wonderful Porch and Parlour cafe in North Bondi to quickly do my morning face - and sneak in a coffee (and croissant), of course.
