### Kate Waterhouse is one of those girls who always looks radiant. She has flawless creamy skin that is a result of eating well, staying out of the sun and exercising. Of course now that she’s pregnant, she is glowing more than ever. When we shot her in her beautiful beachside apartment for Beauticate, we asked her for a detailed rundown on her skin routine. Here, in her bedroom, she shows us how she preps her skin when she’s going out.
