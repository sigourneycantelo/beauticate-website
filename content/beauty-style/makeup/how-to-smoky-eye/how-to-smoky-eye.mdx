  - question: "How do you do a quick smoky eye?"
    answer: "Start with a cream eyeshadow for depth of colour, then blend outward with your fingertip or a brush. Layer a kohl pencil along the lash line and smudge it into the cream shadow. The key is blending -- the more you blend, the more seamless and sultry the finished look."
  - question: "What products do you need for a smoky eye?"
    answer: "You need a soft kohl pencil like Clinique Kohl Shaper for Eyes, a cream or shimmer eyeshadow such as Chantecaille Iridescent Eyeshadow, and a highlight shade like Stila Kitten. A small angled brush helps with precision, and a blending brush softens any harsh edges."
  - question: "Can you do a smoky eye with brown instead of black?"
    answer: "Absolutely. A chocolate smoky eye is softer and more wearable than black. Use a brown kohl pencil like L’Oreal Colour Riche in Enigmatic Brown as your base, layer with warm-toned cream shadows, and blend well. Brown tones suit a wider range of skin tones and are perfect for evening."
