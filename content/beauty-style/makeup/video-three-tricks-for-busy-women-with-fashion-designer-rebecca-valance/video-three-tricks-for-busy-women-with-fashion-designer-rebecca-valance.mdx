There's no denying, fashion designer Rebecca Vallance is one put-together woman. And, to our delight, is a self-confessed makeup addict. But, don't think this busy mum and business owner has all the time in the world to primp... She achieves her look with some nifty little beauty tricks, using just four products. When we recently went to visit at her amazing home, she revealed her three top tips for getting a contoured look that is easy and not over-done.
