So brace yourself for the most efficient, cheat-worthy, time-saving beauty hack ever.  Sigourney has a technique for lining the eyes that is so simple you don’t even need a mirror.  It’s perfect if you’re heading out straight from work and there’s no time to labour over your eyeliner. You could even do it in the back of a cab (- just make sure you’re stopped at the lights, first…)
