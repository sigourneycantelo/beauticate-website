faqs:
  - question: "What sunscreen is best for skiing and snow holidays?"
    answer: "The article recommends layering two sunscreens for maximum protection against the combination of sun glare off reflective snow and harsh winds. Ultra Violette Clean Screen containing zinc oxide with calming green tea, cucumber and rosehip is applied first, followed by IT Cosmetics CC Cream, which is also broad spectrum SPF 50+ with both zinc oxide and titanium dioxide."
  - question: "How do you keep your skin hydrated in the snow?"
    answer: "Start with a pre-trip facial if possible - the La Prairie Art of Luxury Pop Up at Sydney Airport offers facials, hand massages and eye treatments before your flight. During the trip, use a thick moisturiser like Weleda Skin Food at night, add a hydrating face mask such as Minenssey Hydrating Clay Mask with a few drops of flannel flower oil, and apply rich body cream like Kiehl's Crème De Corps to protect from cold and wind."
  - question: "What beauty products should you pack for a Queenstown or snow trip?"
    answer: "Essential items include a broad spectrum SPF (doubled up), a lip balm with SPF such as Moo Goo Natural Lip Balm SPF 15, a nourishing bath treatment to prevent the hot-bath dehydration trap, and rich overnight skincare. Hand cream and cuticle oil are also important, as cold air is particularly drying on hands."
  - question: "What is the Spire Hotel in Queenstown like?"
    answer: "The Spire is a boutique hotel right in the centre of Queenstown village, praised for spacious rooms with balconies overlooking snow-capped mountains, a roaring fireplace, and an Eames Chair. Staff remember guests by name and go the extra mile, which is exactly the personal touch that makes boutique hotels worthwhile when travelling far from home."
