### The great thing about modeling from an early age is access to a steady stream of makeup artists. Jessica Gomes starting modeling before she was at school, and the 29-year-old is now an expert on all things makeup. From DIY primer to the best way to use your lash curler lashes, she reveals a step-by-step guide to getting the Gomes glow.
