### Australian-born makeup artist Sarah Jagger has been based in London for the past 18 years. She is a regular fixture on morning shows giving her commentary on makeup, and has finessed the faces of stars like Reese Witherspoon and Mischa Barton. Here, she shows us how she does her own makeup in eight minutes or less. Hint: it’s all about a glowy, peaches and cream complexion, chocolate-rimmed eyes and nude lips.

_Makeup by [Sarah Jagger](http://www.sarahjagger.com/); Directed by Erica Bergsmeds; Edited by Alistair Martin, William Wong, Jake Dyson._
