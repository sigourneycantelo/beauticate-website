#### \#\#\# Despite its pared back, relaxed, ‘I woke up like this’ effect, the no-makeup look isn’t always easy to pull off. From too little coverage wasting away throughout the day, to knowing when to stop, it’s a tricky art – but, one well worth mastering. Busy mum and Beauticate founder, Sigourney, knows too well the desire for quick and easy barely-there makeup - so she pulled together the best products for a natural finish, and gives you a step-by-step on exactly how to apply. Gigi-esque skin, pronto.
