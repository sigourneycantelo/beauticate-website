  - question: "How are lash extensions applied?"
    answer: "Lash extensions are applied individually by a trained technician who bonds each synthetic lash to a single natural lash using a special adhesive. The process typically takes one to two hours for a full set, and the results look natural and polished."
  - question: "Can lash extensions help prevent fine lines?"
    answer: "Yes, lash extensions can actually help prevent premature fine lines around the eyes. Because they eliminate the need for daily mascara application and removal, you avoid the repeated tugging and rubbing of the delicate eye area that can contribute to wrinkles."
  - question: "How often do you need lash extension refills?"
    answer: "Most people need lash extension refills every two to three weeks to maintain a full, polished look. Regular fortnightly appointments keep the lashes looking their best as natural lashes shed and new ones grow in."
  - question: "Are lash extensions worth it for busy women?"
    answer: "Lash extensions can significantly cut down your morning makeup routine by eliminating the need for mascara and eyelash curling. They keep you looking perennially put together and polished, which is especially valuable for women with busy schedules."
