### Sally Obermeder, is one busy lady. But you’d never know it by looking at her skin. As a host of Seven’s [The Daily Edition](https://au.tv.yahoo.com/the-daily-edition/) and founder of her own website [Swiish](http://www.swiish.com.au/) and ambassador for new skincare product [Renu 28](http://www.asea.net/en-au/whatwedo/renu28.aspx), (as well as being a mum to a two year old), Sally has had to hone her skincare habits into a very speedy, yet effective routine. Here she shows us her five steps to getting a camera-worthy complexion. Get ready for your close up.
