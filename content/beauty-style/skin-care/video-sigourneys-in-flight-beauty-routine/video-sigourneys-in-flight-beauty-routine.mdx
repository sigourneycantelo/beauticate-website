#### Despite what celebrities would have you think (as they saunter off their private jet, flawless), there is generally nothing pretty about a long haul. Combatting skin drying air compression, travelling germs and less than perfect REM sleep is no easy feat, but there are tips and tricks to getting from take-off of to tarmac relatively fresh. Beauticate founder, Sigourney, recently flew to the South of France with Emirates and took the opportunity to give you a rundown of her in-flight beauty ritual - and it doesn't involve pestering your aisle buddy with multiple spritzes of facial spray...
