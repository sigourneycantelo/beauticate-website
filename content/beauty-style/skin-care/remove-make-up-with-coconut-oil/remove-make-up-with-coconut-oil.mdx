  - question: "Is coconut oil safe to use as a makeup remover?"
    answer: "Yes, coconut oil is safe and effective for removing makeup, including stubborn eye makeup. It's a natural alternative to commercial cleansers that gently dissolves makeup without harsh chemicals. However, those prone to congestion should patch test first, as coconut oil is comedogenic for some skin types."
  - question: "Does coconut oil leave a greasy residue on your face?"
    answer: "Coconut oil will leave some residue after makeup removal, but this is intentional-you can massage it in as a moisturising treatment rather than wiping it off completely. If you prefer a cleaner finish, follow up with a gentle water rinse or a secondary cleanser."
  - question: "Why do you need to warm coconut oil before applying it?"
    answer: "Coconut oil is solid at room temperature, so gently rubbing it between your palms melts it into a liquid that's easier to spread across your face and massage into makeup. This warming step also makes the oil more effective at dissolving makeup without tugging at delicate skin."

#### [Coconut oil](https://www.amazon.com.au/Honest-Goodness-Organic-Coconut-Virgin/dp/B07J215MHQ) is one of the most versatile health and beauty products around... It’s the darling of the nutrition world (an easy to digest source of fatty acids that doesn’t change form when you heat it), is super economical (a giant tub is dirt cheap) and it smells like Fiji. With a multitude of beauty uses, from moisturizing the body to smoothing haywire hair, [coconut oil](https://www.amazon.com.au/Honest-Goodness-Organic-Coconut-Virgin/dp/B07J215MHQ) also makes a kick-ass cleanser, according to Margaret Zhang. “Whenever I’m wearing makeup, I’ll use coconut oil to remove it, because then you don’t have to worry about moisturizing afterwards, and you go straight to bed. It’s so good for your skin and it smells good.” Here’s how she does it…

![Margaret](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/remove-make-up-with-coconut-oil/margaret-1.gif)

**Step 1 –** Scoop a small handful of coconut oil out of the jar. Usually it will be solid, unless it is a hot day.

![Step 2 – Turn it into a liquid by rubbing it gently between your palms](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/remove-make-up-with-coconut-oil/margaret-2.gif)

**Step 2 –** Turn it into a liquid by rubbing it gently between your palms.

![Margaret](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/remove-make-up-with-coconut-oil/margaret-3.gif)

**Step 3 –** Smear it all over a dry face. Massage it into your eyes if you’ve got thick eye makeup on, “and let it sit for a second so everything dissolves,” adds Margaret.

![Margaret](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/remove-make-up-with-coconut-oil/margaret-4.gif)

**Step 4 –** Next, remove it – and your makeup. “Just grab a pad and wipe everything off,” she says.

![Margaret](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/remove-make-up-with-coconut-oil/margaret-5.gif)

**Step 5 –** Next, Then rinse your face with water. There will be some coconut oil residue, just massage it in for a moisturising treatment.
