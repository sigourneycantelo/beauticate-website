Korean beauty, or K-beauty, continues to dominate the skincare world with next-level formulas, skin-healing ingredients, and results you can see. In this curated guide to the best Korean beauty products of 2025, we're showcasing must-try serums, hydrating masks, and more. Whether you're new to K-beauty or a longtime fan, these top picks will take your skincare game to the next level.

## Laneige Water Sleeping Mask

**Best overnight mask for deep hydration.** Known for its overnight hydrating power, this mask leaves the skin refreshed and dewy by morning. Enriched with minerals and botanical extracts, it's perfect for dry or stressed skin.

*Perfect for:* Adding hydration to your night-time skincare routine.

## COSRX Advanced Snail Mucin Essence

**Top pick for skin repair and glow.** Packed with snail mucin, this essence is a K-beauty favourite for its ability to deeply hydrate, repair, and improve skin texture, leaving a smooth and plump appearance.

*Perfect for:* Those who need extra nourishment and enjoy lightweight, hydrating layers in their skincare.

![COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/ulta-media-re0ifvfn8u8lq7rslp2nze9ndrbvh97gcb82qp1or8.jpg)

## Innisfree Green Tea Seed Serum

**Best antioxidant serum for dewy skin.** This antioxidant-rich serum has a lightweight texture that quickly absorbs into the skin, making it a hit for all skin types. The green tea extract helps to maintain moisture and deliver a healthy, radiant glow.

*Perfect for:* Day-to-night hydration that leaves your skin with a fresh, dewy finish.

![Innisfree Green Tea Seed Serum](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/39dba63454d8dfeacd06d4a060175a67-re0ig6ppiuo1ljberty6tbf6idsa1mg8dv1wi0kyok.jpg)

## Missha Time Revolution Essence

**The K-beauty dupe for high-end radiance.** Often compared to high-end essences, this cult-favourite from Missha helps boost skin radiance, improves texture, and enhances hydration. It's enriched with fermented yeast extract to strengthen the skin barrier.

*Perfect for:* Skin that needs an extra boost in hydration, texture, and radiance.

![Missha Time Revolution First Treatment Essence](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/83b32ab83950c85fbbde8201affa56ba-re0igg43f70wtmxr8y0gi91sg8hy6lhjr5kras70yc.jpg)

## Dr. Jart+ Cicapair Tiger Grass Treatment

**Best colour corrector for redness.** This colour-correcting treatment is ideal for redness-prone skin, calming irritation while providing light coverage. The green-to-beige colour transformation blends effortlessly for a flawless look.

*Perfect for:* Redness-prone or sensitive skin that needs a soothing, corrective product that doubles as light makeup.

![Dr. Jart+ Cicapair Tiger Grass Colour Correcting Treatment](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/87f008baf5fba1fb4414286e4e519cd3-re0igqgcq86xirc6neo2cng7myuex4v4qvufp9py1g.jpg)

## Banila Co Clean It Zero

**Best cleansing balm for makeup removal.** This sherbet-like cleansing balm is a K-beauty staple, melting away makeup and impurities without stripping the skin. It's gentle, effective, and perfect for those who love double cleansing.

*Perfect for:* Anyone who wears makeup regularly and wants a hydrating, thorough cleanse at the end of the day.

![Banila Co Clean It Zero Cleansing Balm](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/79b455a8e0436e5d08f02922f76eb572-re0ih0sjljt7x23pw6y913txipo0vxrn5zxfuvcd5g.jpg)

## Sulwhasoo First Care Activating Serum

**Best luxury serum for radiance.** This luxurious serum from Sulwhasoo uses traditional Korean herbs to enhance radiance, balance, and hydration. It's a favourite among those looking for anti-aging benefits.

*Perfect for:* Mature skin or those looking for a luxurious first step in their skincare routine to enhance overall skin health.

![Sulwhasoo First Care Activating Serum](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/bfb723bc0541f4b7732d92faf4b5b1f8-re0ihb4roq7dgrop7tf5aj801y928lwovf3s4wx190.jpg)

## Etude House SoonJung pH 5.5 Toner

**Best soothing toner for sensitive skin.** With a low pH and soothing ingredients like panthenol and madecassoside, this toner is perfect for sensitive or compromised skin, helping to calm irritation and provide gentle hydration.

*Perfect for:* Sensitive skin that needs a calming, hydrating toner that won't cause irritation.

![Etude House SoonJung pH 5.5 Toner](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/b34fad3d91e24fdeb425d8f09f964575-re5mtrwyhx0ynxujwysa6cew3dyd4h7vmvl8w6yo44.jpg)

## Klairs Freshly Juiced Vitamin Drop

**Best gentle vitamin C serum for brightening.** This gentle vitamin C serum brightens the skin, reduces dark spots, and improves skin tone without the irritation often associated with vitamin C products.

*Perfect for:* Brightening and evening out skin tone, especially for those new to vitamin C.

![Klairs Freshly Juiced Vitamin Drop](/content/beauty-style/skin-care/best-korean-beauty-products-2025/klairs_freshly_juiced_vitamin_drop_35ml_-_hero-r3e0c33tf9qv4a8bcd1k7kf58mrzks4vbdfutbnams.webp)

## TonyMoly I'm Real Sheet Masks

**Best affordable sheet masks for any skin type.** These affordable sheet masks come in a variety of types (like aloe, tea tree, and rice) to suit any skin concern. Each mask delivers hydration and targeted treatment for various skin needs.

*Perfect for:* A quick skin pick-me-up; ideal for pamper days or weekly self-care.
