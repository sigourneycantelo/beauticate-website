  - question: "What is the Mesoestetic Mesoeclat treatment?"
    answer: "It is a three-step medifacial designed to brighten skin by exfoliating outer layers, deeply nourishing and smoothing fine lines. It was developed by the Barcelona-based Mesoestetic Pharma Group."
  - question: "Is the Mesoeclat facial uncomfortable?"
    answer: "There is some mild discomfort during the pre-treatment exfoliant and ampoule phases, which can feel warm on the skin for around 20 to 30 seconds. Most clients find it bearable and the results far outweigh the brief sensation."
  - question: "How many treatments do you need to see full results?"
