## Who is Elly Seymour?

That's Elly Seymour. Before Saint Louve existed, she was a registered nurse and skin specialist with fifteen years in clinical skincare, the kind of experience that means she's seen what a bad ingredient combination does to real skin, repeatedly, up close. She started formulating Saint Louve in 2020, and it took more than two years of development with cosmetic chemists before the range launched. That's not a brand timeline built for a content calendar. That's someone taking her time because she knew what she wanted to get right.

## What is the Saint Louve range like to use?

I tried the whole range, cleanser through to the serums, and the thing that struck me first wasn't a single ingredient. It was how considered the whole thing felt. The packaging is minimal, almost clinical, no wasted flourish, and once you start using the products you realise that restraint runs right through the formulations too. Nothing feels thrown in for the label. Every ingredient combination reads like it was chosen by someone who actually knew what she was doing, because she did.

The Facial Cleansing Oil is the one I've reached for most. It's the kind of cleanser that makes you reconsider what a cleanser is for, properly dissolving the day rather than just foaming it away, and my skin never feels stripped afterwards. The Facial Cleansing Gel is in the rotation too, a lighter, second-step follow-up rather than a stand-alone strip-everything-back cleanse.

## Which niacinamide serum is worth knowing about?

Then there's the serum everyone should actually know the name of: niacinamide (that's Vitamin B3), panthenol (Vitamin B5) and hyaluronic acid, layered together in Saint Louve's Vitamin B3, B5 & Hyaluronic Acid Hydrating Serum. If you've searched "niacinamide serum" before landing here, this is the one worth knowing about. It's not a flashy formula, it's a genuinely well-built one: independent ingredient analysis from [Skincarisma](https://skincarisma.com/products/saint-louve/vitamin-b3-b5-hyaluronic-acid-hydrating-serum) rates it Excellent, 98 out of 100, and lists it as fragrance-free and reef-safe, with an ingredient list that looks well suited to dry and sensitive skin. That's not brand copy. That's an outside body checking the ingredient list line by line and coming back impressed.
