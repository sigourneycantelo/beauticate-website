#### _We already know that red wine is an essential for getting through a long day. But, your glass of Pinot Noir is also essential for beauty, beyond the 'antioxidant = good skin = free reign vino'. Although (no more than) a daily glass of red wine (more so than white) has health and skin benefits, the reason has been isolated to a very important ingredient: resveratrol, found in the skin of grapes. The ingredient, now being used in skincare, is showing itself to be a saviour for a sallow, ageing complexion and is said to improve elasticity, firmness and radiance. Here, we share with you the best resveratrol products on the market. Say hello to your new skincare secret weapon..._

At $119, the price point is a little exxy, but it’s hard working formula makes it a sound investment if dry skin is your main beauty problem.
