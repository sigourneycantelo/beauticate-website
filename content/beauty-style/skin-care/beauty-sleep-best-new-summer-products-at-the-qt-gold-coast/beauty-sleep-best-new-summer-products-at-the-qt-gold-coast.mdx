Welcome to Beauticate’s newest series: Beauty Sleep. Filmed in some of our favourite hotels and resorts, these videos are all about checking in and checking out. It's so important to set aside time for yourself and this series celebrates those moments. This week I'm checking in to the upbeat QT Hotel on the Gold Coast and working my way through my favourite new summer essentials - from the cult cool-girl sea salt spray to a delicious coconut-scented salt soak and everything in between. Tune in to find out exactly how to perfect the tousled waves and gilded glow you’ve been coveting.
