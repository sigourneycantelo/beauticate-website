faqs:
  - question: "What is the skin barrier and why is it important?"
    answer: "Your skin barrier is like a protective wall made of skin cells (bricks) held together by lipids, oils, ceramides and hyaluronic acid (cement). A healthy barrier prevents moisture loss, protects against irritants and pathogens, and maintains hydration. When damaged, it leads to dryness, sensitivity, irritation and premature ageing."
  - question: "How do ceramides help with dry and sensitive skin?"
    answer: "Ceramides are a critical component of your skin's protective cement layer. People with dry skin and eczema often have naturally reduced ceramide levels, causing skin cells to separate and lose moisture. Using ceramide-rich skincare products helps restore barrier function, lock in hydration and reduce sensitivity and irritation."
  - question: "What damages the skin barrier?"
    answer: "Common culprits include harsh exfoliators with strong acids, over-cleansing, hot water showers, and irritating fragmented skincare products. Even well-intentioned treatments like frequent glycolic peels can damage your barrier if not balanced with hydrating, gentle products. Minimising these practices helps maintain a healthy, resilient skin barrier."
  - question: "Should I use simple or complex skincare for a damaged barrier?"
    answer: "Dermatologists recommend keeping your routine simple when your barrier is compromised. Fragrance-free, minimalist formulations with derm-approved cleansers and moisturisers containing ceramides are preferable to complex, heavily fragranced products that can further irritate and sensitise skin."
