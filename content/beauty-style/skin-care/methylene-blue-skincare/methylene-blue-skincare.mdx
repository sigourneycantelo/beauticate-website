## What is methylene blue, and why are biohackers taking it?

Which is how I first came across methylene blue. Not on a serum label. In a biohacker's supplement stack.

Unlike many of these breakthroughs, methylene blue is ancient by wellness standards. It's a compound that's been used in medicine since the 1890s. The biohacking crowd has been taking it for a while now for mitochondrial energy, focus and cellular longevity.

Worth being clear about what that actually is. Taken internally, methylene blue is a prescription-only medicine in Australia, and the biohacking use of it is off-label. That's a conversation for them and their doctor, not a suggestion from me. What has turned up in my bathroom is something quite different: a topical cosmetic serum, applied to the face, not swallowed.

So it's no surprise to me that the skincare industry has caught on. Ingredients don't usually stay in one lane for long, and the fact that methylene blue targets your mitochondria and fights oxidative stress at a cellular level is what skin ageing is about too.

## What does the science actually say about methylene blue for skin?

There's a [2017 study out of the University of Maryland](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-02419-3) that backs this up properly. In lab-grown 3D skin models it increased collagen and elastin activity, improved hydration and boosted dermis thickness, without irritating the skin even at high concentrations.

Researchers also found methylene blue outperformed other well-known antioxidants at protecting skin cells from oxidative stress.

It's cell-level in-vitro research rather than a clinical trial on human faces, but it's real science, not just an ingredient riding a trend with nothing behind it.

## Why USP grade methylene blue matters

The methylene blue in it is USP grade, which stands for United States Pharmacopeia, the body that sets purity standards for pharmaceutical ingredients. Given how easily methylene blue moves through skin, that purity matters and is the difference between a properly manufactured active and something closer to a lab reagent.
