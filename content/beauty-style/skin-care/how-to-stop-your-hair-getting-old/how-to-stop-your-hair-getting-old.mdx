faqs:
  - question: "Why does hair change texture and lose volume as we age?"
    answer: "Research by Unilever has found that hair follicles become smaller with age, which reduces volume and lipid oil production. The result is dull, dry and flat hair."
  - question: "What foods help slow hair ageing?"
    answer: "Salmon is ideal - it is packed with protein and Omega 3 fatty acids, both building blocks of healthy hair. Yoghurt adds vitamin B5 which increases blood flow to the scalp, while nuts provide good fats."
  - question: "Does colouring hair make it age faster?"
    answer: "Yes. Chemicals like peroxide strip the lipid layer from the hair shaft and create damage. Staying as close to your natural shade as possible minimises that damage."
  - question: "What supplements can help with thinning or fine hair?"
    answer: "Options worth trying include Blackmores Hair, Skin and Nails (contains biotin, silica and zinc), QSilica (mineral silica in water for better absorption) and Viviscal (protein-based with zinc and biotin)."
