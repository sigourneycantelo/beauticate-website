#### If you’re sick of trying to choose between the standard bright coral vs red toenail shade at your local mani/pedi place, then get ready to shake things up a bit. Sigourney shot this tutorial on how to do a gold foil pedicure using Jamberry Nail Wraps (when she was still so pregnant she could barely reach her toes). It’s super simple (just heat them up and stick them on) and you don’t have to sit around waiting for your toes to dry. It's the perfect accompaniment to a bit of Sunday night Netflix. Just add your basic manicure tools and you’re ready to pedi.
