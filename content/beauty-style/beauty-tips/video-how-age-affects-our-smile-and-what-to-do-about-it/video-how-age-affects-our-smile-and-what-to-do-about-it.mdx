Heard of the Mesial Drift? Neither had we. It’s something that happens to our teeth from the age of 20, wreaking havoc in the mouth. Sigourney sits down for a conversation with [Dr. Gamer Verdian](https://www.instagram.com/drgamerverdian/?hl=en), the founder of [The Dental Lounge in Double Bay](https://dentallounge.com.au/) and Sydney CBD.

Known for his transformative smile makeovers and expertise in microscopic dentistry, Dr. Verdian approaches his craft with a unique blend of artistry and cutting-edge technology. Recognized as having the 'best eyes' in the business, he is a world leader in the Academy of [Microscope Enhanced Dentistry](https://microscopedentistry.com/) (AMED).
