  - question: "How do you recreate Taylor Swift's tousled low bun from the Look What You Made Me Do video?"
    answer: "Curl medium-width sections of hair with a flat iron or curling wand, twisting each in a different direction for a natural finish, then set with a light hold spray. Gather hair loosely at the nape, wind into a bun and pin it, letting a few tendrils fall at the sides, then sweep the fringe diagonally across the forehead and secure with a pin."
  - question: "How do you get Taylor Swift's wet-look hair?"
    answer: "This look works best on second-day hair that is not freshly washed. Lightly dampen the hair, divide it into three or four sections and finger-comb a mousse through from root to ends, then scrunch to create body and wave. A soft gel can be added to enhance the wet appearance, but the hair needs a thorough cleanse afterwards."
  - question: "How do you create the perm-style curly look from Look What You Made Me Do?"
    answer: "Use a small curling wand to twist around 1cm width sections in different directions, then scrunch through with a lightweight mousse and lift hair at the roots for added body. The look works naturally for those with curls, and pairs well with a bold dark lip for evening wear."
