### At Beauticate, we can’t go past a beauty-fest, so we were thrilled when Sigourney was asked to take part in [Westfield’s Beauty and Wellness Weekend](http://westfield.com.au/beauty-wellness) happening on October 15 & 16. As part of the shopping giant’s new beauty campaign, The Front Line (led by our girl crush and former Beauticate-interviewee [Robyn Lawley](https://www.beauticate.com/who/robyn-lawley-model)). The weekend will take place in 19 Westfield centres across Australia and there everything from beauty masterclasses to free yoga sessions and healthy juice bars. Sigourney will be hosting two beauty workshops at Westfield Belconnen in Canberrra and you can head to the [Westfield Belconnen website](https://www.westfield.com.au/belconnen) to find more details. To celebrate the campaign she went shopping at four of her favourite retailers in Westfield – [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com/au/), [L’Occitane](http://au.loccitane.com/), [Jurlique](http://www.jurlique.com.au/) and [Chanel](http://www.chanel.com/en_AU/) and made a video about her favourite ways to decompress.
