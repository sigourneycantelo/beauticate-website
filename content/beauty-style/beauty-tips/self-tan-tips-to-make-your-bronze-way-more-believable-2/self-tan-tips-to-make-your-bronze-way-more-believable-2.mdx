    answer: "Gradual tanners are ideal if you're new to self-tanning, as they're nearly impossible to mess up.   - question: "How do you avoid streaks and orange palms when self-tanning?"
    answer: "Always use a velour tanning mitt to apply your self-tanner-it prevents product from getting on your palms and helps you see exactly where the product is going. You can erase streaks as you apply, ensuring an even, flawless finish without the orange-handed look."
  - question: "What should you do to prepare your skin before self-tanning?"
    answer: "Exfoliate your skin thoroughly before applying self-tanner to ensure an even application. Complete any hair removal at least a day beforehand. Apply moisturiser to drier areas like elbows, knees and knuckles to prevent the tan from grabbing or staining those areas."
