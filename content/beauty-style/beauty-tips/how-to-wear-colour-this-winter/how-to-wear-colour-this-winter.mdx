#### _The chill is finally descending, although that's not a reason to banish your colourful pieces to the back of the wardrobe just yet. We get it, you're mourning the loss of summer, but that doesn't mean you have to start wearing all black. Amidst a sea of oversized, chunky knits; thick denim; and long coats; colour is your new best friend. There are many (surprisingly easy) ways to incorporate brights, so go to your closet, part the sea of black sweaters, and tell yourself that you can do this: you can add colour to your winter wardrobe. Here are our tips on brightening up your look this season, no matter the budget…_

## Wrap up in a Coat…

Rug up in a ruby-toned coat. Pair it back with neutral colours to make it the statement piece in your look. With so many great shades around, you’ll bound to find a coat in the right colour for you.

## When in Doubt, Pout it Out…

You can never go wrong with a good lipstick. If you’re not confident about coloured clothing (or even if you are) a bold lippy is always a good choice. Pick a vibrant shade, like plum, to  compliment any outfit and paint a selfie-worthy pout.
