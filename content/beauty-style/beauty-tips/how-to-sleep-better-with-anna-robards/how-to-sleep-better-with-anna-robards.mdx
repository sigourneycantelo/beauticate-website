#### If there’s one thing Anna Robards (nee Heinrich) knows about, it’s sleep. The lawyer turned model turned entrepreneur is co-founder of Intu Wellness, a sleep and skin supplement that has the health and influencer industry buzzing. Not only did she totally slay on SAS she’s also married to actor, chiro and biohacker Tim Robards, so between the two of them, she’s extremely well versed in the science of sleep. Sigourney and Anna recently got together at her glamorous home in Sydney’s Eastern Suburbs to talk through their bed-time rituals and shared what actually works for them.
