Some of our favorite Aussie fashion designers share their personal beauty tips and tricks.

## BRIDGET:

### "I keep my face routine really simple.

I use Kiehl’s Cleanser, Toner and Moisturiser. Then I put the Nars Pressed Powder on my face to matte things down and then the Nars Laguna for a bit of bronze. I love the Hourglass brushes, I use them for my tinted moisturiser, pressed powder and bronzer. For eyes, I’m a big fan of the By Terry products to get rid of the dark shadows and then their [mascara](http://mecca.com.au/by-terry/mascara-terrybly/V-010705.html?cgpath=brands-terry-makeup-eyes#start=1) as well."

## NICK:

### "I don’t do heaps [in terms of grooming].

Probably Kiehl’s Ultra Facial and that’s about it for me. Every now and again I put product in my hair, I don’t even know what it’s called! I’m pretty low maintenance. I actually go to the barber, a fairly traditional one, I basically say, ‘tidy it up’ haha! I’ve always used the Issey Miyake Summer fragrance, but I’m having trouble finding it these days, it’s the one in the flat bottle, I don’t know whether it’s a limited edition but I generally only find it when I travel. Gascoigne and King supply our stores so they always send us the new candles to test, we’re not into incense or smoke so it’s a nice sort of way to have essential oil."

## KARLA:

### "I believe you should age gracefully but do what’s in your power to slow the process.

So, I use Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream, Powerful Strength Line Reducing Concentrate & Clearly Corrective Dark Spot Solution. At night I use Estee Lauder Advanced Night Repair. When it comes to makeup, I can't go past Laura Mercier Tinted Moisturiser for day and Giorgio Armani Foundation for night. I religiously use cold pressed sesame oil all over (trust me, your skin will never feel softer!)."

## CANDICE:

### "I like and appreciate everything about the beauty process.

I work with amazing hair and makeup artists who have beautiful recipes for different looks. I loathe that I cannot commit enough time to focusing on my own beauty routine as I often have a two-year-old running around the house with an arm full of my brushes and creams.

### I shower and wash my face with Go-To Properly Clean Cleanser…

… and moisturise with the Melissa Day Cream from Dr Hauschka. I also use [Palmers Coconut Oil](http://bit.ly/2kU5rPO). I use Giorgio Armani Luminous Silk Foundation, a non fancy but faithful blush I can’t remember the name of, ModelCo Mascara and, depending on how I am feeling, a matte shade of lipstick in either red or soft pink."
