### Be it the smell of woody pine needles, shop windows laden with gaudy tinsel or the onslaught of Christmas-inspired fodder – Santa Claus is most definitely coming to town. However the Christmas to-do list can be ever daunting: decorating the tree, hanging the wreath, filling the fridge, and of course – Christmas Shopping. The late night dash to crammed and crowded shopping centres can be a most traumatic experience for anyone (one which renders a definite "eek!"). For a shop sans le stress, the click-click-and-voila method of online shopping seems a most desirable option.

![Photo by John French](/content/beauty-style/beauty-tips/the-beauty-holics-guide-to-christmas/john.jpeg)

###### Photo by John French.
### In between the bon bons and baubles, we have donned our chicest of Santa hat’s (fortuitously red is in vogue), adorned the tip of the tree with an Angel and laid out milk and cookies for the big man in the hat, as we unwrap our Beauticate Advent Calendar. For those who have been nice this year, there are glorious gifts for all just a hop, step and jingle away.
