2016 has seen an array of cool beauty trends take our feeds by storm; there was strobing, baking, liquid lipstick, maximalism, ‘no makeup’, and Korean beauty products, to name a few. But one of the craziest trends we’ve come across (and are yet to brave) is ear makeup.

EN-US

JA

From the Louis Vuitton runway, it seems all the bright young things have given this trend a go. Metallic paints and inky blacks have been popular (see Taylor Hill) as well as basic white. If you’re feeling game, a wash of rose gold might make the perfect accompaniment to your glam New Years Eve outfit, or maybe a hint of gold is perfect for Christmas Eve drinks. Just take a heavily pigmented eye shadow and brush and paint away.
