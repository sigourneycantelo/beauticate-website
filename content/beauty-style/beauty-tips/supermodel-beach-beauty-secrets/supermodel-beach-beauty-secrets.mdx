### As a certain literary genius once wrote, summer’s lease hath all too short a date. Vibrant colours and laid-back feelings, warm sandy skin and cool water swims inspire more than just Shakespearean sonnets. Yes, it’s the season of reverie and the season we romance about, so soak it up like Helena, Linda, Claudia and Kate and indulge in the temporal beauty of summer, supermodel style.
