What's the best styling tool for fine hair? A multi-styler that dries, curls and smooths in one go, like the Dyson Airwrap, tends to outperform any single straightener or dryer, especially when it's paired with a proper wash day and scalp routine. Below is the full edit, including exactly how to use each tool for maximum volume.

A hairstylist once told me that the secret to creating lasting volume on fine hair was selecting the right tools. She also cautioned that fine hair is difficult to style and that products promising glossy locks can actually make fine hair limper. She was right. Tools that worked beautifully for friends with medium to thick hair left my hair flat and frizzy. After a lot of trial and error, this is the current line-up that guarantees a good hair day, refreshed for 2026 with a few new additions worth knowing about.

## Wash day reset: set the foundation for volume

Before any tool comes near your hair, volume starts in the shower. The [Christophe Robin Cleansing Volumising Paste with Rose Extracts](https://beauticate.shop/products/christophe-robin-cleansing-volumising-paste-pure-with-rose-extracts-75ml) turns into an airy foam that lifts hair at the root while it cleanses, so you're not fighting flatness before you've even picked up a styling tool. Follow with a lightweight conditioner through the mid-lengths only, skip the roots.

Then, before you style, mist through the [Christophe Robin Instant Volumising Leave-In Mist with Rose Water](https://beauticate.shop/products/christophe-robin-instant-volumising-leave-in-mist-with-rose-water-150ml) on damp or dry hair. This step is non-negotiable for me. It gives roots that lifted look and adds a bit of natural hold before any heat goes near your hair, which makes every tool below work harder for you instead of the other way round.

## The Dyson Airwrap Co-anda2x: why I use it every day

I've tested a lot of styling tools over the years, but the [Dyson Airwrap Co-anda2x](https://myer.sjv.io/MKga4P) is the one that's earned a permanent spot on my counter and I reach for it daily. It's Dyson's most powerful Airwrap yet, built around the new Hyperdymium 2 motor, which pushes twice the air pressure of the original for faster drying and better curl hold. It's also the lightest Airwrap Dyson has made, which matters more than you'd think when you're holding it up for twenty minutes at a time.

The part that's actually changed my routine is the intelligent side of it. The i.d. curl sequence uses Bluetooth to read your hair type and automatically adjusts heat, airflow and timing for you. It also knows when to release each section itself, so you're not yanking hair out before it's ready.

My best tip for fine, flat hair specifically: pin curl as you go. Curl each section with the barrel, then pin it up against your head and let it cool completely before you take it down. It sounds fussy but it's the one thing that's actually made a difference for me. Heat sets the shape, but it's the cooling that locks it in, so if you release it too early, you lose the volume before you've even left the bathroom.

I filmed myself using an earlier version of the Airwrap on my own kids for the video below. The styling principles carry across both versions, so it's still the best way to see the attachments in action before you commit.

Fine hair often comes down to what's happening at the scalp, not just the ends. Follow with the [OiTO GROW Scalp Elixir](https://beauticate.shop/products/grow-scalp-elixir-30ml), a trichologist-formulated blend of jojoba, rosemary and cedar oils, massaged into a dry scalp for ten minutes before washing. It's not an overnight fix, but consistent use is where the real volume gains happen.
