  - question: "How do you use a curling tong without burning yourself?"
    answer: "The key is technique and cord management. Hairstylist Paloma Rose Garcia demonstrates the simplest method: work in sections, keep the tong angled away from your face, and wrap hair around the barrel rather than clamping from the tip. Practising the correct wrist position prevents burns on the forehead and fingers."
  - question: "What is the best curling tong for beginners?"
    answer: "A standard barrel curling tong, such as the BaByliss model featured in this tutorial, is ideal for beginners because the clamp helps hold the hair in place. Look for a tong with an even heat distribution and adjustable temperature settings. Pair it with a heat protector product to minimise damage."
  - question: "Should you blow-dry hair before using a curling tong?"
    answer: "Yes, hair should be completely dry before using a curling tong to avoid heat damage and achieve a lasting curl. Blow-drying first with a good hairdryer like the Parlux 3800 also adds body and smoothness. Applying a styling product beforehand, such as a Kerastase treatment, helps the curls hold their shape longer."
