#### We love this new Instagram-inspired trend that will make your after-shower ritual feel extra fabulous. Although you may not want to leave the house in your terry cloth towel, there’s no harm in playing a little dress up. Especially if it’s for your next ‘gram.

![](https://www.beauticate.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Bathleisure4-1.jpg)

First of all, what is “bathleisure”? The term seems to spring from a British Vogue edition that featured the ever-iconic Rihanna sporting a terry cloth towel around her head on the cover. Meanwhile, the rest of her outfit was as high-fashion as could be. Instagram picked it up quickly, and the recipe seems to go something like this:

![](https://www.beauticate.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Rihanna-1.jpg)

1\. Wrap a terry cloth towel around your head (we love these)

![](https://www.beauticate.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Bathleisure3-1.jpg)

2\. Don either a bathrobe or an oversized men’s shirt

![](https://www.beauticate.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Bathleisure2-1.jpg)

3\. Accessorize to the nines, with sunglasses, earrings, necklaces, and bracelets

![](https://www.beauticate.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Bathleisure7-1.jpg)

4\. Swipe on a [highlighter](https://www.mecca.com.au/yves-saint-laurent/touche-eclat-radiant-touch/V-009111.html), [mascara](https://www.mecca.com.au/too-faced/better-than-sex-mascara/V-016521.html?cgpath=makeup-eyes-mascara#srule=Best+Sellers&sz=12&start=1), and [lip gloss](https://www.mecca.com.au/frank-body/lip-cheek-tint/V-028991.html?cgpath=makeup-lips-lipgloss#srule=Best+Sellers&sz=12&start=1)

![](https://www.beauticate.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Bathleisure5-1.jpg)

5. Optional: grab your favorite mug and have a sip of tea

![](https://www.beauticate.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/Bathleisure1-1.jpg)
