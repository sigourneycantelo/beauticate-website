So many of us are first in line at the salon door, inspo screenshots at the ready, for our stylists to weave their magic creating the latest hair colour trend. The only thing is, as great as that platinum blonde or icy balayage look, the over-processing wreaks havoc on the health of our hair. Others of us simply have naturally dry, unruly hair that seems impossible to tame. So, Beauticate founder, Sigourney, went to Goldwell master stylist Alan White to give us step-by-step tips on keeping porous strands strong, shiny and smooth. Get ready to take notes...
