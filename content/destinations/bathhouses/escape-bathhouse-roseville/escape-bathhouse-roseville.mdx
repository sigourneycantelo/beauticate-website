Cam and Tasch D'Arcy, the couple behind the Mudgee glamping retreat Sierra Escape, found a 1929 Spanish Mission building on the Pacific Highway and turned it into the prettiest daylight bathing on the North Shore.

![Escape Bathhouse, Roseville, heritage Spanish Mission interior with arched openings](/content/destinations/bathhouses/escape-bathhouse-roseville/hero.jpg)

The heritage bones do the heavy lifting. Terracotta tiles, arched openings, barley-sugar twisted columns and enough natural light to make a bathhouse feel bright, which is rare. The circuit runs across two floors: a hot mineral pool at body temperature, a sauna at ninety, a steam room, and ice baths hovering around ten to twelve degrees. Book the two-hour Nunyara ritual for breathwork, exfoliation and a rose-quartz facial. The Placebo Bar pours kombucha and mocktails instead of wine. Beautiful, and better for you than a long lunch.
