Roughly 150 plants share the room with one large Finnish sauna and three ice baths. That is the whole pitch, and it works. Aalto Community sits on King William Road in Goodwood, and it decided early that the point was not exclusivity. There is no membership. You drop in.
Co-founder Tom Emmett keeps coming back to one word, accessibility, whether you are a local or someone's interstate visitor killing a spare afternoon. Warm up, plunge, sit in the foyer with a tea, do it again. The greenery softens everything. A bathhouse that would rather you felt welcome than initiated.
