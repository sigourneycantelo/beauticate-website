Six people. That is the entire capacity at Comma, and the restraint tells you everything about it. Architect Eloise Fotheringham built the Cremorne bathhouse around the idea that solitude is a practice rather than an indulgence, then pared the space back until only the essentials remained. Sauna, soak, sweat, in the Scandinavian order. The lighting is custom Ross Gardam, the Nebulae pendants throwing a soft glow over surfaces kept almost monastic. Robes and towels are laid out, phones are not welcome, and the whole thing runs on stillness. An inner-city bathhouse that behaves like a destination spa an hour from anywhere. Book it for one. That is how it was designed.
