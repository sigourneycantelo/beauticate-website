Madeleine Snaith and Jake Salomone cut their teeth running pilates and yoga studios in Mackay, then came south to Brisbane with a bigger idea. Contro opened on Lutwyche Road in 2024 and everything here is private. No sharing a plunge pool with strangers. You book your own suite and work through it at your own pace.
There are traditional and infrared saunas, mineral soaks, float therapy, steam and salt rooms and a red-light room. The palette is Mediterranean, all soft pastel and curve. It reads calm before you have taken off your shoes. Contrast therapy without the crowd. Rare, and rather clever.
