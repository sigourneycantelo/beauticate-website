There is no chlorine here. That is the whole point. Reine DuBois, who also founded The Health Lodge next door, built the Byron Bathhouse on a naturopath's first rule. Do no harm. So the water is medical-grade and free of the chemicals that dry your skin and catch in your lungs.
What you get instead is contrast bathing done properly. Hot, then cold, then hot again, until your nervous system finally drops its shoulders. It is billed as Australia's first bathhouse of its kind. The claim holds up. Come for the ritual. Stay because nothing stings.
