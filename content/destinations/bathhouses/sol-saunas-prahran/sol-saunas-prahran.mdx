Sol was built on an idea the Scandinavians take for granted. That heat is better shared. The Prahran space runs on it, a social sauna clad in charcoal and pale blonde timber where strangers end up talking on the tiered benches and then plunging cold together afterwards. The palette is Nordic. Restrained, warm, unfussy. Sessions move between the sauna and the cold, the rhythm doing the work, the conversation doing the rest. No phones, no performance, just the oldest wellness ritual there is done properly. Connection to yourself and to the people beside you. That is the whole pitch. It holds.
