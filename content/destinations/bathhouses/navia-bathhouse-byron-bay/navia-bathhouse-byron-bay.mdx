The idea started in Denmark, with a word the Danes use for warmth and belonging. *Hygge*. Navia has built the rest around it. Black timber sauna rooms, the traditional rocks, heat that settles into your shoulders. There are hot mineral baths and steam rooms and, when your body asks for it, an individual cold plunge that takes the breath clean out of you. Then a massage room to put you back together.

![Navia Bathhouse, Byron Bay](/content/destinations/bathhouses/navia-bathhouse-byron-bay/hero.jpg)

It sits on Marvell Street, minutes from the water, though you forget the town entirely once you are inside. Nordic ritual, Byron address. You leave loose.
