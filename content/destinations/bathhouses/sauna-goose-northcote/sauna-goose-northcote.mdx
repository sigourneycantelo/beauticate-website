Cara Sealey and Simon Kelada-Polupanov missed the saunas that got them through Copenhagen winters, so they built one on Gadd Street. Sealey, a landscape architect, designed it herself out of wood, raw materials and eclectic second-hand finds, which is why it feels like a friend's very good backyard rather than a clinic. There is a woodfired sauna, a seven-degree cold plunge, a fire pit to gather around and a vegetable garden out the back. The ritual to try is the saunagus, where a gusmester pours water and essential oils over the stones then wafts the aromatic heat toward you. Ten minutes in, ten minutes out. Copenhagen, in Northcote.
