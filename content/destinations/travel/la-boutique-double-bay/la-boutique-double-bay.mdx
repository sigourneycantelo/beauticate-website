Widely known as "The Blonde Whisperer," Belinda Jeffrey is one of Australia's most sought-after hair colourists, with a career spanning more than 30 years across film, fashion, editorial and advertising. Her client list reads like a who's who of Australian and international celebrity — Sarah Murdoch, Miranda Kerr, Russell Crowe, Leonardo DiCaprio and Carey Mulligan among them — while her editorial credits include campaigns for *Vogue*, *Harper's Bazaar* and *Vanity Fair*.


La Boutique, her salon now located at 36 Kings Lane in Darlinghurst (having recently relocated from its longtime Double Bay address), is an extension of Belinda's philosophy: unpretentious, high-energy and unapologetically luxe. The interiors are finished in gold, rose gold, platinum and bronze, with a champagne cabinet on hand for good measure. Together with a handpicked team of senior colourists and stylists, La Boutique delivers colour without equal.
