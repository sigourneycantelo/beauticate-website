Ten villas on a ridge above the Indian Ocean. That is the whole property. Adults only, no crowds, no lobby. Each villa has a heated plunge pool, a fireplace, a private deck and views that run to the horizon line. The nearest neighbour is the surf.
The day spa sits at the edge of the retreat, treatment rooms facing the water. iKOU botanicals, infrared sauna, full-body exfoliations and couples treatments that make use of the view. Afterwards you walk back to your villa along a bushland path, heat up the plunge pool and watch the light change.

![Twin treatment beds facing the ocean through open glass doors at Injidup Spa Retreat's day spa](/content/destinations/travel/injidup-spa-retreat-yalingup/injidup-spa.jpg)

Cape Naturaliste is the quieter end of the Margaret River region. The Cape to Cape track runs nearby. World-class breaks sit below the cliffs. The wineries are a short drive south. But the real pull is the retreat itself, the kind of place where you arrive planning to explore and end up staying put. The ocean does most of the work.
