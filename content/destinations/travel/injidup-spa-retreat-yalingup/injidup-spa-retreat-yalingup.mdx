Perched on a rugged stretch of Western Australia's Cape Naturaliste coastline, Injidup Spa Retreat is an adults-only sanctuary of just ten private villas, each with panoramic Indian Ocean views and a heated plunge pool. Set along the Margaret River wine region's acclaimed Cape to Cape track near Yallingup, the retreat offers an intimate escape where the bushland meets the sea.


The onsite day spa specialises in restorative wellness journeys — from infrared sauna therapy and full-body exfoliations to signature couples treatments using iKOU botanical skincare — all performed in treatment rooms overlooking the ocean. Villas feature fireplaces, kitchenettes, private decks with barbecues and freestanding baths in the honeymoon suite. Nearby, guests can explore world-class surf breaks, award-winning wineries and the historic Caves House Hotel.
