  - question: "What treatments does Youth Lab in Perth offer?"
    answer: "Youth Lab in West Perth offers advanced dermal therapies including Hydrafacial, Healite II LED light treatment, injectables, dermal fillers and medical grade light-based treatments. The clinic is led by Medical Director Dr Kate Jameson and uses products from Ultraceuticals, Cosmedix and Mesoestetic."
  - question: "What is a Hydrafacial and what does it do?"
    answer: "A Hydrafacial is a multi-step facial treatment that cleanses, exfoliates and extracts impurities using a Vortex-Fusion serum delivery system. It deposits medical-grade serums into the skin at each step while simultaneously removing dead skin cells, excess oil and congestion. The treatment is painless, requires virtually no downtime and finishes with a dose of antioxidants and hyaluronic acid for hydration."
  - question: "What is Healite II LED treatment?"
    answer: "Healite II is an LED light therapy that enhances the body's natural cellular recovery processes. It improves healing time, promotes skin rejuvenation and can help subside active acne. At Youth Lab, it is typically used as a follow-up to a Hydrafacial for maximum results."
