The thermal suite at Noosa Springs takes its cue from Roman bathing, which sounds like a stretch until you are in it. There is a hydromassage pool with jets strong enough to knock the week loose. A heated salt-water flotation pool where an hour is said to rest you like six. A thermal capsule that packs you in warm chocolate clay.

![Noosa Springs Spa, Noosa Heads, thermal suite](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/noosa-springs-spa/hero.jpg)

The spa sits inside the golf resort at Noosa Heads and the products lean organic, Eminence and the local Okeoni line. Book a package and give it the full afternoon. Steam, float, treatment, courtyard, repeat. You will leave loose-limbed and faintly smug. That is the correct response.
