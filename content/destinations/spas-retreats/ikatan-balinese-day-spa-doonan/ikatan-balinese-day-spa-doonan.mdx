Turn off the road at Doonan and the Noosa hinterland does the rest. Ikatan sits on acreage minutes from the coast, built as a series of garden pavilions in traditional Balinese style. Water features, lush planting, timber that has weathered into the landscape.
You treat here in the open, surrounded by the gardens, which is the whole point. The therapists work in the Balinese tradition, long massage rituals and facials using natural and organic products. There is a garden lounge and a library for the slow part afterwards. It has been a Noosa institution for years and it earns the title. Bali without the flight. Almost.
