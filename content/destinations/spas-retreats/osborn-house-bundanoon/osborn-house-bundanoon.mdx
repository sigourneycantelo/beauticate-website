The estate has stood at Bundanoon since 1892, which in Australia counts as ancient. Osborn House wears it well. The spa runs the full sequence. Sauna, steam room, infrared, then LED to finish the skin off properly. Outside, a twenty-five metre heated pool and a jacuzzi look straight out over Morton National Park, all that green falling away beneath you. Book the Forest Lodge and you get your own deck with a sauna, an ice bath and a fire pit, forest bathing without leaving the property. It is the Southern Highlands doing what it does best. Old money calm. Cold-plunge nerve.
