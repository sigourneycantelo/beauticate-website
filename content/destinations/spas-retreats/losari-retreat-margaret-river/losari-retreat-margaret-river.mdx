Sixty-six acres, eight minutes from town, and six villas set far enough apart that you forget the others exist. Losari is a private estate in the Margaret River wine country, the kind of place people book for a milestone and then refuse to leave.

![Losari Retreat private villa with lake views, Margaret River](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/losari-retreat-margaret-river/hero.jpg)

The garden spa villas are pavilion-style with two bedrooms and a lake to look at. Every villa has its own outdoor jacuzzi, so the spa is not a room you walk to. It is on your deck. Book a massage and a therapist comes to you. There is a tennis court if you must move. There is an eco-fireplace for when you would rather not. Stay two nights. Cancel your plans.
