Nestled within the tropical grounds of Cable Beach Club Resort & Spa in Broome, Chahoya Spa is a sanctuary where the pace of the Kimberley quietly takes hold. The name means "pamper" in Japanese — a fitting designation for a retreat that draws on healing philosophies from around the world.

![Chahoya Spa treatment room](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/chahoya-spa-by-loccitane-broome/chahoya-treatment.jpg)

Seven private treatment suites, including a double Vichy shower room, set the stage for signature rituals such as the Kimberley Dreamtime and Chahoya Pearl Massage, each designed to harness the restorative energy of Broome's rugged coastal landscape. Facials use LaGaia Unedited's pH-balanced formulations, while body therapies incorporate local-inspired touches like ground lapis lazuli and lemon myrtle. Adults only, by appointment, and best booked at the time of your resort reservation — this is slow, intentional wellness at the edge of the Indian Ocean.
