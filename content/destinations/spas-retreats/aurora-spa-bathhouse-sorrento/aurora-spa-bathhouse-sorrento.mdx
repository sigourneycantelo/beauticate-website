#### Tucked beneath the sandstone facade of the [InterContinental Sorrento](https://sorrento.intercontinental.com/aurora-spa-bathhouse), the reimagined Continental Hotel that has anchored this stretch of the Mornington Peninsula since 1875, [Aurora Spa & Bathhouse](https://auroraspa.com.au/) is the kind of place that makes you want to cancel whatever you had planned for the rest of the day.

Part of the hotel's sweeping AU$120 million redevelopment, the spa occupies a vast 1,000 square metres of subterranean space that feels both grand and intimate at once. Descend the stairs and the temperature shifts, the light softens, and the outside world, the limestone cliffs, the bustling village, the car park squabble, simply ceases to exist.

![The entrance to Aurora Spa & Bathhouse at InterContinental Sorrento](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/aurora-spa-bathhouse-sorrento/aurora-exterior.jpg)

The bathhouse is the centrepiece, and it is worth arriving early to give it the time it deserves. Four hydrotherapy pools sit at varying temperatures, designed to be moved through in sequence: warm to hot, cold plunge, then back again. The circuit is meditative and quietly addictive, each transition sharpens the senses before the next pool lulls them back into submission. Between dips, there are steam rooms and thermal experiences to explore, and a relaxation lounge where you can stretch out with a cup of tea and stare at nothing in particular, which is rather the point.

![The hydrotherapy pools at Aurora Spa & Bathhouse](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/aurora-spa-bathhouse-sorrento/bathhouse-pools.jpg)

![The subterranean thermal circuit at Aurora Spa Sorrento](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/aurora-spa-bathhouse-sorrento/bathhouse-thermal.jpg)

The treatment rooms, eight in total, are hushed, beautifully appointed spaces. Facials, massage and body treatments all draw on the house [ASPAR](https://aspar.com.au/) product line, Aurora's own range of botanically-led formulations that smell like a very expensive garden. The products are elegant without being fussy, and the therapists apply them with the kind of unhurried confidence that tells you they genuinely believe in what they are using. For something more immersive, the float room offers a sensory deprivation experience that strips away every last thread of tension, not for the claustrophobic, perhaps, but deeply restorative for everyone else.

![A treatment room at Aurora Spa & Bathhouse](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/aurora-spa-bathhouse-sorrento/treatment-room.jpg)

![Inside the bathhouse at Aurora Spa Sorrento](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/aurora-spa-bathhouse-sorrento/bathhouse-interior.jpg)

What elevates Aurora beyond a hotel day spa is the sense that every detail has been considered with real intention. The architecture honours the heritage of The Continental while the interiors feel unmistakably contemporary. There is no muzak. No synthetic lavender assault on entry. Just clean lines, warm stone, and the gentle echo of water moving through the pools below.

The setting helps, of course. Sorrento is one of the Peninsula's most charming villages, and the InterContinental has positioned itself as its anchor, a place where a weekend away can pivot effortlessly between long lunches at the local restaurants, coastal walks, and an afternoon dissolving into the bathhouse. Aurora Spa is rated number one in Sorrento on TripAdvisor, which feels deserved. It is the rare spa where you leave feeling not just pampered but genuinely recalibrated.

ASPAR products are available to purchase at the spa and [online](https://aspar.com.au/), so there is no excuse not to extend the ritual at home. Though frankly, a jar of body oil in your bathroom will never quite replicate the experience of floating weightless in a subterranean pool while someone else worries about dinner.
