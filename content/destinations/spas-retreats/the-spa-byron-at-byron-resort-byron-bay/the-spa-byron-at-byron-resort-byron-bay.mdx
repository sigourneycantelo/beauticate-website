Nestled within 45 acres of subtropical rainforest on the outskirts of Byron Bay, Eléme Day Spa is the wellness sanctuary at Crystalbrook Byron, a responsible luxury resort near Tallow Beach. Formerly part of the Byron at Byron Resort, the property is now under the Crystalbrook Collection, whose sustainability-first ethos extends to the spa's approach: treatments are built around natural botanicals and the healing energy of the surrounding rainforest.


The menu is designed to flex, allowing therapists to personalise each session — from deep-tissue massage and infrared sauna to bespoke facials using plant-based formulations. Guests can extend the experience with complimentary morning yoga, heated infinity pool access and rainforest boardwalk walks. Open Monday to Saturday, Eléme is both a resort amenity and a destination day spa for visitors to the Byron hinterland.
