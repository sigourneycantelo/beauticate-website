## Beachside Skin Clinic in Scarborough

#### Serene Day Spa sits on Scarborough Beach Road, on the coast north of Perth, and is both a day spa and a skin clinic, with a team of dermal and beauty therapists working across both halves. On a hot Perth summer's day, walking into it is its own argument.

Floor to ceiling windows flood a contemporary space with light. Designer armchairs and sofas in shades of grey fill the lounge, where clients wait with herbal tea. Then a dimly lit corridor and a plush bed, and the temperature drops about ten degrees in every sense.

## The Consultation

Treatments start with a therapist going through your skin and what you want from the appointment, and the treatment gets chosen from there rather than from the menu.

## The A-Zyme Peel and Microdermabrasion

A representative course of action is an Ultraceuticals A-Zyme Peel with microdermabrasion.

It opens with a double cleanse using the Ultraceuticals Ultra Balancing Gel Cleanser, then the microdermabrasion, which is done first so the peel that follows can get further in.

The A-Zyme peel combines Retinol and Bromelain. It has a distinct aroma, strong but not unpleasant, and a light prickly feeling across the skin that settles quickly once the therapist starts infusing it with the Ultraceuticals sonophoresis device.

Then another gentle cleanse, an infusion of Ultraceuticals vitamins B2 and C, and the Red Action Moisturiser. It finishes with a neck and shoulder massage, which is the part where the day spa half of the business shows its hand.

## The Peeling Week, Which Is Real

You leave with a homecare regime and a range of Ultraceuticals products to keep it going, and the therapists are strict about following it.

Then comes the part worth planning around. Light peeling tends to start around the chin and mouth over the first few days, move to the nose and forehead, and clear by about day five. Book accordingly, and not in the week before anything photographed.
