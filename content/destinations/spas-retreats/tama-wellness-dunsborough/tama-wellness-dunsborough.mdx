You will find it behind Bungalow Social, tucked off Naturaliste Terrace in the middle of Dunsborough. Tama is small and deliberate. The heat comes from an infrared sauna rather than the usual hot rocks, a gentler kind of warmth that lets you stay in longer and think less.

![Tama Wellness intimate studio space, Dunsborough](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/tama-wellness-dunsborough/hero.jpg)

Around it sit treatment rituals and meditation, the slow end of wellness rather than the athletic one. This is not a plunge-and-post-about-it operation. It is a place to go quiet for an hour on your way through wine country. Open Tuesday to Saturday, late morning to mid-afternoon. Book ahead. The room is intimate by design.
