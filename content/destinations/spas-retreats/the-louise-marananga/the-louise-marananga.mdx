The massage comes to you. At The Louise the treatment happens in your suite, which means you can walk ten steps and fall into bed afterwards rather than driving home through the Barossa dusk.

![The Louise, Marananga](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/the-louise-marananga/hero.jpg)

The lodge sits on Seppeltsfield Road in Marananga, vines running to the edge of the property and an outdoor pool that looks straight out over them. It is a Baillie Lodges address, so the standard is high and quietly held. Private yoga on the lawn, Indian head massage, a fitness room with hot and cold sauna. Wine country tends to undo people. This is where you get put back together.
