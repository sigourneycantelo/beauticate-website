Kinara means moon in Pitjantjatjara, and the spa is built as a wiltja, a traditional shelter, dropped into the spinifex with Uluru in full view. Every treatment opens with a Welcome ceremony.
From there the menu runs on native botanicals, Kakadu plum, quandong, desert lime and desert salts, with Irmangka-irmangka at its heart. That healing balm is made from Scented Emu Bush by the women of the NPY Women's Council. This is bush medicine with a lineage, not a marketing line. You lie back, the red sand goes on forever, and the country does most of the work. Nowhere else in Australia treats you quite like this.
