There is nowhere else like it. Behind the white portal doors on Oxford Street is a hush closer to a monastery than a spa, with sculptural egg-shaped pods you climb inside to be reborn and a spiral staircase that belongs in a gallery. Jeannie Bourke has run it for the better part of thirty years, and her people stay for twenty. That tells you most of what you need to know.

![Venustus, Paddington, sculptural spa interior with white portal doors](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/venustus-paddington/hero.jpg)

Each day opens with sage and palo santo. The massages are the thing. Four hands, sometimes six, moving at once. Lymphatic drainage, intuitive work, packages named 11 11 and 777 for anyone who wants to disappear for an afternoon. The skincare is Venustus's own, made in-house. It has a following that reportedly includes Nicole Kidman, though the staff are far too discreet to say. You go in wound tight. You come out rearranged.
