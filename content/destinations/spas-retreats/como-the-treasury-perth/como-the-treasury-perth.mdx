Housed within Perth's beautifully restored 19th-century State Buildings, COMO The Treasury is a multi-award-winning five-star hotel that pairs heritage grandeur with contemporary design by Kerry Hill. At its heart lies COMO Shambhala Urban Escape, a holistic wellness sanctuary where ancient Eastern and Western healing traditions converge.


Set within the building's reimagined subterranean vaults, the spa features four treatment rooms — three singles and one double — a 20-metre heated indoor pool, rooftop fitness centre, and sauna. Treatments span massage, facials and rejuvenating therapies designed to restore balance. The hotel holds a 2025 Michelin Key, was named Australia's Leading Boutique Hotel at the 2024 World Travel Awards, and ranked number one City Hotel and Hotel Spa in Australia by *Travel + Leisure*. COMO Shambhala is open to both hotel guests and day visitors.
