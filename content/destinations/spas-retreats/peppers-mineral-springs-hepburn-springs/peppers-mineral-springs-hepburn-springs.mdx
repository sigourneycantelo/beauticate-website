Nestled within the grounds of the historic Mineral Springs Hotel in Hepburn Springs, The Mineral Spa is one of Australia's most acclaimed natural mineral spas. Architecturally designed to reflect its bushland surrounds, the sanctuary is suffused with natural light and the soothing sound of flowing mineral water, with views stretching across manicured gardens to the countryside beyond.

![The Mineral Spa retreat building set within bushland, Hepburn Springs](/content/destinations/spas-retreats/peppers-mineral-springs-hepburn-springs/mineral-retreat.jpg)

The centrepiece is The Retreat, an intimate indoor-outdoor mineral water bathing zone featuring two outdoor mineral spas, a cold plunge pool, lavender sauna, steam room and sun deck. Seven spacious treatment rooms, including four dual-therapy suites and a double hydro-bath room, host signature treatments by Pevonia Botanica and Sodashi, delivered by therapists selected for their healing hands. A destination day spa in the heart of Victoria's spa country, it draws visitors seeking the restorative power of the region's celebrated mineral springs.
