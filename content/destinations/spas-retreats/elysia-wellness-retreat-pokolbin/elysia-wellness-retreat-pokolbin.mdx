For years this was the Golden Door, the retreat people checked into when they needed to be quietly saved from themselves. Minor Hotels took it on in 2019 and made it Elysia. The bones are still extraordinary. Twenty-six treatment rooms, Vichy showers, Watsu pools where you float while someone moves you through warm water. There is a garden that grows what the kitchen serves, and the kitchen serves nothing with red meat or alcohol or caffeine or sugar. Two hours from Sydney, low among the Hunter vines. You arrive frayed. That is the point. Give it three days.
