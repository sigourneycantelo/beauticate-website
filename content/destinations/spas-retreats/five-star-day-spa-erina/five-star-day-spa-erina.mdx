### Tucked into a plaza off the busy Central Coast Highway at Erina is an institution devoted to downtime, and it has been tending to the local collective dermis for over 14 years. French-inspired interiors, glittering chandeliers, inky walls. Five Star Day Spa takes its namesake seriously, but the thing it's actually serious about is skin.

## Nicole Spargo

The owner is Nicole Spargo, which is a name that rather commits a person to this line of work. She was an account manager in the beauty industry before taking the spa over from its previous owner, and the first thing she did was bring Ultraceuticals in as the professional range.

"It's such a beautiful Australian-made skincare line and it works really well," she says. "Before I worked for Ultraceuticals, I came from another skincare line and I was seeing what the industry was wanting. Clients are wanting fast results."

## The Facial Menu

The signature facial is the Ultraceuticals one, and the interesting part is what can be added to it: microdermabrasion, LED light, peels, A-zyme treatments, and an ultra sonophoresis machine, a low-frequency ultrasound tool used to help product penetrate.

The add-ons are optional for a reason. If your skin is mid-recovery from something else, laser included, the facial runs perfectly well without any of the machinery.

## The Signature Facial

The treatment rooms are opulent, and the facial itself is a long sequence of silky lotions, warm compresses and hypnotic massage, running about an hour.

Arrival is dimly lit, warmly staffed, and starts in the relaxation room with a cup of T2 Strawberries and Cream. Which matters more than it sounds if you have driven the windy coastal road to get here.

## Beyond Facials

Eight treatment rooms, and beyond the facials there are manicures and pedicures, body scrubs, massage, and a wet room with steam and a vichy shower. That last one is a weekend proposition rather than a school-hours one.
