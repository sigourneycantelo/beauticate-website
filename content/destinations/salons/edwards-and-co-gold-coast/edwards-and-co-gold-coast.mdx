## Above Bumbles, Facing the Water

Edwards and Co took over the first floor at 4/19 River Drive and reworked it into the industrial-meets-airy look the group is known for. Large panel windows run the length of the room and face Budds Beach, so the space stays bright through the day, and the walk up from Bumbles Cafe below is part of the appeal.

## The Colour

Jaye Edwards built the business on lived-in colour - the kind that grows out without a hard line - and soft, undone waves. The Gold Coast team works across cutting, colour, blonding, balayage and extensions. Edwards is based in Sydney but comes up to the Gold Coast salon every few months.

## Booking

Appointments run through the Edwards and Co booking system. The salon is at 4/19 River Drive, Surfers Paradise, on (07) 5526 9888.
