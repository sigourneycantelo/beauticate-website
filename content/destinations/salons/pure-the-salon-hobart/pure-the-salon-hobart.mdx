There is a garden growing up the window. At Pure, on Elizabeth Street in Hobart, a vertical wall of green climbs the glass, and it sets the tone for everything else.
Natural timber, natural light, products chosen for how little they cost the planet. The salon calls itself the most pure and natural hair experience in Tasmania, and it has built the room to back the claim. This is hair care for people who read the ingredients list. Come for a colour, leave feeling like you have spent an hour somewhere that breathes. Foliage and foils. An unlikely pairing that works.
