In 2021 this Kingston salon was named Australian Salon of the Year, which tells you most of what you need to know before you sit down. The wash chairs recline. The fit-out was built with sustainability in mind, and Bond runs with Sustainable Salons to keep foils, hair and offcuts out of landfill.

![Bond Hair Religion, Kingston](/content/destinations/salons/bond-hair-religion-kingston/hero.jpg)

It is a full colour, cut and styling house with a following that treats it like a second religion, hence the name. The awards have kept coming since. Come for the colour. Stay because someone finally listened to what you actually wanted.
