Paloma Roan moved out of its Woolloongabba flagship in 2020 and set up on Montague Road in West End, where the light does half the work. The salon is all plants and sun, botanical and a little bohemian, and it has the sustainability credentials to back the aesthetic. A Sustainable Salon partner. Named by Harper's Bazaar as one of Australia's best sustainable salons.
The specialty is lived-in colour. Soft blondes, warm coppers, balayage that grows out like it was always meant to. Ethical extensions too. It is a creative space as much as a salon. Go for the colour. Stay for the calm.
