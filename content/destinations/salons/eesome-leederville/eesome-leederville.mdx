Electric Lane is one of those Leederville side streets that rewards people who look up from their phones. Eesome sits along it, a hair salon done in the pared-back, light-filled style the suburb does so well. White walls, good light, nothing fighting for your attention while you sit in the chair.

![Eesome hair salon on Electric Lane, Leederville](/content/destinations/salons/eesome-leederville/hero.jpg)

The focus is the work rather than the theatre, which is increasingly rare and increasingly the point. Book a colour or a cut and settle in. It is the kind of room where an afternoon goes quickly. Find the lane. Then find the door.
