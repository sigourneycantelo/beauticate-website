Half the space is a hair salon. The other half is a designer fashion boutique, and the line between them is deliberately blurred.

![De Stijl Hair, Hobart](/content/destinations/salons/de-stijl-hair-hobart/hero.jpg)

De Stijl runs as an Aveda concept salon on Elizabeth Street, which means the colour is vegan and the ethics come standard. It hangs like a gallery, clothes and styling stations sharing the same considered light. Owner Tracy Snow has been named a state hairdresser of the year finalist more than once, so the work behind the aesthetic holds up. Get your colour done, then browse the racks while it develops. Two good reasons to go, under one roof.
