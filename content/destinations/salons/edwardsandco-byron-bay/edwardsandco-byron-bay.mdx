Jaye Edwards built a salon group that turned hairdressers into names people follow. The Byron outpost keeps that up. On the floor you might find Clayton Truefitt, a master stylist with fashion-week and editorial runs in Melbourne and London behind him, or Stephanie Clifford Hosking on colour.
It sits in Centennial Circuit, in the industrial pocket of town, open Tuesday through Saturday. This is the kind of place you book before a wedding or a big birthday, or simply when you want your hair to look like it belongs to a slightly more organised version of you. Byron gloss. Big-city hands.
