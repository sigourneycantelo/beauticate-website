Paloma Rose Garcia spent sixteen years in fashion, including a stint with Hermes and fifteen Australian Fashion Weeks, before turning the old Oscar Oscar space on Oxford Street into something closer to a curated hotel. Polished wood floors, muted tones, fresh flowers and copies of Monocle and The Gentlewoman set the tone.

![Paloma, Paddington, polished wood salon interior with muted tones](/content/destinations/salons/paloma-paddington/hero.jpg)

The welcome ritual runs to Beauty Chef elixirs and matcha, served in Maison Balzac carafes, because the details are the point. The signature is the lived-in cut, worth its reported two-month wait, and Kevin Murphy colour led by the blonde whisperer Ash Croker. Undone yet elevated. You will think about the scalp massage for days.
