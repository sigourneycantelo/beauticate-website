Brad and Sharon Rixon opened their first chair in 2000 and have been colouring Brisbane's best heads ever since. The Edward Street salon, up on level one, won Best Salon Design in 2018 and you can see why. Blackwell's Design Co took the industrial shell and softened it with sculptural stone, so the room feels raw and considered at once.
There are more than fifteen stylists and colourists on the floor. Blondes, balayage, the lot. It is the sort of salon where the fit-out matches the work, which is not always a given in this city. Book the good one. Book early.
