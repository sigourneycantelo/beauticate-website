## Australia's Original Brow Artist

Amy Jean is known in the trade as the Arch Angel, which is the sort of nickname you only get by being the one everybody else copies. The business, as she puts it, is dressing the eyes, and she's done it for Dannii Minogue and Delta Goodrem among others.

## Inside the Sydney Salon

The salon is small and dark-walled, with a chandelier, a gold plaque above the desk reading Amy Jean Eye Couture, low lighting and a vase of red roses. The whole room smells of caramel, which is her own Palazzo candle in Coconut Splice doing its work. There are fashion books to flick through and a shelf of what she actually uses at eye height: Dr Spiller skincare, Priori Lash Recovery Serum, the Eye Couture range, and a row of very soft makeup brushes.

Above the register, a top shelf of Bollinger. Read into that what you like.

## The Art of Dressing the Eyes

"I believe eyes are windows to the soul," Amy Jean says. "I love to create and I've always been a keen artist." The comparison she draws is to couture: read the face first, then build the brows and lashes to frame it, rather than applying one shape to everybody.

Getting an appointment with her personally takes months. She trains her staff in her own technique and coaches them closely, which is the whole reason the other salons work.

The treatment room is white and light-filled, papered with pink flamingos, with white roses spilling out of a vase in the corner.

## What's on the Menu

Brow shaping and the Korean Lash Lift, which perms the lash so it stands up on its own and tints it, doing away with mascara for about eight weeks. Then Mist Brow Tattoo, the refined evolution of Feathertouch and the option for sparse or patchy brows, Keratin Brow Lamination, Kiss Me Lip Tint, Forever Lash Liner, areola tattooing, LED light therapy and Pico laser removal.

Every service ends with a makeup touch-up from the Privee Collection, so you leave without the telltale post-wax redness.

## Worth Knowing

Brow tattooing isn't performed during pregnancy. Worth checking before you book if that's what you're coming in for.

Prices vary by service and aren't listed here. Bookings for all salons go through [amyjean.com.au](https://amyjean.com.au/pages/make-a-booking), and the Sydney flagship is on 0447 167 227.
