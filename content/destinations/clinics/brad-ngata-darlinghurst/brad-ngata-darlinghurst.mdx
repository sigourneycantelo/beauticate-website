Two-time Australian Hairdresser of the Year, Brad Ngata has spent more than two decades building one of Sydney's most respected independent salons. Tucked into the creative, Soho-like stretch of Darlinghurst Road, Brad Ngata Hair Direction operates as a one-on-one, single-operator studio — no production line, no battery-hen formula.

![Brad Ngata Hair Direction's plant-filled Darlinghurst studio with vintage mirrors and styling chairs](/content/destinations/clinics/brad-ngata-darlinghurst/brad-salon.jpg)

Brad's editorial credentials span shoots for *Sunday Times Magazine* and *Culture Magazine*, plus advertising campaigns for Nokia, with named collections including Rebel, *Tangeres*, Breakage and Paper Cut showcasing his fashion-forward range. Inside, full-length mirrors, cascading greenery and gold-rimmed porcelain lend a sense of playful luxury that clients consistently describe as both stimulating and surprisingly peaceful. Recognised at Hair Expo and the Maybelline Australian Fashion Awards, Brad also holds back-to-back titles for Sydney's Best Hairdresser.
