  - question: "What is a Visia facial imaging system?"
    answer: "Visia is a futuristic 3D facial imaging machine that takes several photos to reveal skin concerns including pigmentation, texture and dehydration, often showing issues that are not visible to the naked eye. Skinologie Albert Park uses it as part of their initial consultation to understand each client's skin before recommending any treatment."
  - question: "What is the difference between melasma and UV-related pigmentation?"
    answer: "Melasma is a type of pigmentation caused by hormonal factors, often triggered or worsened during pregnancy, while UV pigmentation is caused by sun damage. The Visia at Skinologie can distinguish between the two, which matters because some treatments suitable for UV pigmentation are not appropriate for melasma without proper skin preparation."
  - question: "What is sonophoresis and how does it benefit the skin?"
    answer: "Sonophoresis is an ultrasound instrument used to dramatically amplify the penetration of skincare actives, such as antioxidants, into the skin. At Skinologie it is used after a lactic peel to push a potent antioxidant cocktail deeper into the skin than topical application alone would achieve."
  - question: "What is a lactic acid peel and is there any downtime?"
    answer: "A lactic acid peel is a gentle, hydrating chemical exfoliant that sloughs off dead skin cells and plumps dehydrated skin. The Skinologie version has no downtime - the reviewer left with fresh, renewed skin and no redness or recovery period required."
