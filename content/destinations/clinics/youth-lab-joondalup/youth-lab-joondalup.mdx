## Perth's Growing Appetite for Skin Treatments

#### [Dr Kate Jameson](/interviews/founders/dr-kate-jameson-cosmetic-physician/dr-kate-jameson-cosmetic-physician) opened [the first Youth Lab in West Perth in 2018](/destinations/travel/the-youth-lab-perth/the-youth-lab-perth), a second in Claremont in 2020 that opened into COVID lockdowns and survived them, and this third one in Joondalup. Perth, she says, is a city that keeps asking for more of this.

"The demand for cosmetic procedures is steadily growing here in Perth," she says. "Much of this can be attributed to our harsh climate, proximity to the equator, clear skies and our outdoor lifestyle. We have significant UV exposure."

## Another Gurtler Interior

Nickolas Gurtler did this one too, sprawling across a sunlit space in Joondalup's CBD. Slate-hued walls, gold hardware, Dina Broadhurst on the wall and a reception desk made from polished Venetian plaster set with Arabescato Vagli marble.

![Kaye Hr](/content/destinations/clinics/youth-lab-joondalup/kaye_4304-2_hr-2.jpg)

The waiting area runs to curved bouclé chairs and Slim Aarons coffee table books, and an iPad for your details, though you won't get far into the Slim Aarons before someone comes to collect you.

![Kaye Hr](/content/destinations/clinics/youth-lab-joondalup/kaye_4298_hr-1-1.jpg)

## The First Visit

Every first-timer gets a full consultation before anything else: photographs, an assessment, a skincare plan. This is the part that separates a clinic from a salon, and it's arguably worth more than whatever treatment follows it.

![Kaye Hr](/content/destinations/clinics/youth-lab-joondalup/kaye_4412_hr-1.jpg)

## The Advanced Facial

The Advanced Facial is customised at the bed. If you're already running strong actives at home, the therapist swaps the exfoliation for an enzyme rather than a peel, which is the sort of adjustment you want a clinic to make without being asked.

From there, a sonophoresis machine to drive the Mesoestetic products in, then a long massage.

## The LED and the Hydrafacial

The LED is the [Lutronic Healite](https://www.youthlab.com.au/treatment/healite-ii-led-perth/), an 830 nanometre yellow light. The [Hydrafacial](https://www.youthlab.com.au/treatment/hydrafacial-perth/) is the clinic's most-booked treatment and the one they point new clients to first.

There's also [a range of lasers](https://www.youthlab.com.au/2019/03/19/2019-3-9-the-facts-and-guide-to-cosmetic-laser/) across the group, which is the reason most people end up making a second appointment.
