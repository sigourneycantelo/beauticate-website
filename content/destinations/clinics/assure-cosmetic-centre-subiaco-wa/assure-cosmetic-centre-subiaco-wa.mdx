## Surgical and Non-Surgical Under One Roof

Tucked away in leafy Subiaco, [Assure Cosmetic Centre](https://www.assurecosmeticcentre.com.au/) is deliberately discreet about what is a very broad practice: six surgeons, cosmetic nurses and specialist dermal therapists, covering peels and paramedical facials at one end and surgical rejuvenation at the other.

## The Room

Minimalist, white-toned and, well, clinical. Which sounds like a criticism and isn't. There's no attempt to dress it up as a day spa, and the effect is a room that looks like it knows what it's doing. Fresh flowers and comfortable furniture in the waiting area, and SkinCeuticals behind glass along one wall.

![Assure Cosmetic Centre Treatmentroom](/content/destinations/clinics/assure-cosmetic-centre-subiaco-wa/assurecosmeticcentre-treatmentroom-1.jpg)

## Who's Here

The surgical team is Dr Brigid Corrigan, Dr Mark Hanikeri, Dr Bernard Luczak, Dr Rohan Page, Dr Vij Vijayasekaran and Dr Anthony Williams.

On the dermal side, Anna Robinson has been with Assure since 2009, Caitlyn since 2017 and M.A since 2022. Anna assesses a face properly before she starts, making notes and asking questions while her hands work over the face, neck and *décolletage*, firm but never rough.

![Anna](/content/destinations/clinics/assure-cosmetic-centre-subiaco-wa/anna.jpeg)

## The SkinCeuticals New York Facial

The best known treatment on the non-surgical side stacks microdermabrasion, a peel, a mask and LED light therapy into one appointment, finishing with a serum and a sunscreen. It's done on a contoured bed under cotton blankets thick enough to undo most of the clinical impression the room worked so hard for.

## Why the Mixed Team Matters

The structural point is worth understanding, because it's the actual reason to choose a practice like this one.

"There is such a benefit to working in a team with medical professionals," Anna says. Dermal therapists and prescribers work in the same building, so anything needing a prescriber is handled here rather than referred out, and the two sides can plan around each other. Whether any prescription treatment is appropriate is a question for a consultation, not a listing.

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [assurecosmeticcentre.com.au](https://www.assurecosmeticcentre.com.au/contact/) or (08) 9380 0380. You'll find it at Suite 216, 25 McCourt Street, Subiaco. Surgical and injectable treatments start with a consultation.
