Take the lift to level five of the Hunter Mall Chambers, a heritage building in the middle of Newcastle, and you arrive somewhere unexpectedly serene. Coco is the Artisan group's Newcastle clinic, styled coastal-modern to nod at the beach town outside its windows. The device list is serious. Halo, Clear and Brilliant, Pico, Thermage, BBL, HydraFacial and RATIONALE facials for the days you want to be gentler about it. Dermatologists, cosmetic nurses and dermal therapists run the floor, so nobody is guessing. This is proper clinical skin, softened at the edges. Heritage ceilings above. Real science below.
