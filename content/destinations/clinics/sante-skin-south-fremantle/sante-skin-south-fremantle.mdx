Fewer treatments, greater care. That is the line Sant&eacute; leads with, and in an industry that tends to upsell, it lands like a small act of rebellion. The South Terrace boutique is run by two registered nurses, Rachel Mackin Brown and Ashlea Treasure, both trained in cosmetic nursing and dermal science.

![Sant&eacute; Skin boutique clinic on South Terrace, South Fremantle](/content/destinations/clinics/sante-skin-south-fremantle/hero.jpg)

The menu is deliberately short. Injectables, skin needling, medical-grade peels, LED, a good facial. What you will not get is a long list of things you did not need. The room is minimal and considered, the conversation honest about what is worth doing. This is the anti-overhaul. You leave looking like yourself, only better rested.
