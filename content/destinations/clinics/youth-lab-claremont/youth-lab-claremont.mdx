#### Youth Lab opened [its West Perth clinic](/destinations/travel/the-youth-lab-perth/the-youth-lab-perth) in 2018 and followed it into the western suburbs with this one, a heritage building a stone's throw from the centre of Claremont.

## The Nickolas Gurtler Fit-Out

The space was conceived by Melbourne designer Nickolas Gurtler, who also did the Nedlands showroom for cult furniture purveyor James Said. The brief was the understated end of New York's Upper East Side, and the good decision was to leave the building alone where it was already interesting: lofty ceilings, intricate plasterwork, modern pieces set against them rather than over the top of them.

![Youth Lab Ng Dr Lr](/content/destinations/clinics/youth-lab-claremont/20201123_youthlab_ng_dr-71_lr.jpg)

Inside the door, a table of Jonathan Adler vases under an angular light fitting. The waiting room turns on an artwork by [Dina Broadhurst](/interviews/creatives/dina-broadhurst-artist/dina-broadhurst-artist). Calacatta Viola marble and brass fittings run through the treatment rooms.

![Youth Lab Ng Dr Lr](/content/destinations/clinics/youth-lab-claremont/20201123_youthlab_ng_dr-107_lr.jpg)

## Dr Kate Jameson

[Dr Kate Jameson](/interviews/founders/dr-kate-jameson-cosmetic-physician/dr-kate-jameson-cosmetic-physician) and her team run clinical dermal therapy and injectable treatments across both clinics. The Hydrafacial is one of the better known things they do.

## The Signature Facial

The [Signature Facial](https://www.youthlab.com.au/treatment/advanced-facials-perth/) is the house treatment, pulling elements from several others into one appointment. It opens with a consultation, then an ultrasonophoresis cleanse, then a bespoke enzymatic peel with the therapist watching to judge whether the skin will take a second layer.

Then a long massage, a Mesoestetic Radiance DNA Crystal Fibre Mask, and the Healite II LED to finish.

## On the Way Out

You're handed a glass of water spiked with The Beauty Chef's Hydration Booster, which is the beauty-from-the-inside-out philosophy delivered in a glass and, more to the point, cold.
