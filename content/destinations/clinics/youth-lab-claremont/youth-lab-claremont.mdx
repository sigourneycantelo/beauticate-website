After opening their well-loved West Perth clinic in 2018, Youth Lab are now extending their reach into the city’s western suburbs with their elegantly designed Claremont outpost. A stone’s throw from the heart of Claremont, it’s a chic urban oasis where modern design perfectly and respectfully complements the heritage of the building that houses the clinic.

Where state of the art treatments and unabashedly luxurious design combines.
