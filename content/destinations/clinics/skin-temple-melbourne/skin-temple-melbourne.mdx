### The address says St Kilda Road but you go in from Arnold Street, which sets the tone. Skin Temple is a medispa in the literal sense, five treatment rooms and a tiled Turkish hammam sharing a floor with a clinical skin menu, and it's considerably bigger than it looks from the door.

![ ambience is the call of the day at the chic medispa   ](/content/destinations/clinics/skin-temple-melbourne/img-13.jpg) ambience is the call of the day at the chic medispa

## Inside the Turkish-Inspired Medispa

The waiting area is white walls, gilded mirrors, one very large armchair and a run of tea light candles leading you to reception. There's a bathroom to change in, waffle robes and slippers, and treatments start with your feet in a copper bowl of warm bubbles before you get anywhere near a treatment bed. The beds are heated. It's a lot of spa apparatus for a clinic that does actual clinical work, which is rather the point of the place.

![ instant calm, the moment you step through the door ](/content/destinations/clinics/skin-temple-melbourne/img-14.jpg) instant calm, the moment you step through the door

## Dr Alicia Teska

Skin Temple is presided over by cosmetic physician Dr Alicia Teska, working with a team of dermal therapists across the clinical side of the menu.

## The Pigment Regulating Facial

The pigmentation treatment is a SkinCeuticals one, and it's an active. High concentrations of glycolic and salicylic acid, which is worth knowing before you book it in as a relaxing hour.

It opens with a cleanse and exfoliation, then an examination under a magnifying lamp, which is also where you'll hear how much of your pigmentation is sitting below the surface rather than on it. The mask is mixed for you at that point. Expect tingling and tightness while it's on, and expect to be asked about it repeatedly, because the therapists check in the whole way through. There's a neck and arm massage while it works. Then it comes off, a serum and moisturiser go on, and you're handed a cup of tea and a rose water Turkish delight while you come back to earth.

![ creature comforts while you wait ](/content/destinations/clinics/skin-temple-melbourne/img-15.jpg) creature comforts while you wait

## What to Know Before You Book

You go home with a three-day course of skincare, sunscreen and a tinted moisturiser included, and a written order to use them in. Redness and some shedding over the following days are part of how the treatment works. Staying out of the sun for the week afterwards isn't optional.

It can't be done on anyone pregnant or breastfeeding. And it isn't the treatment to book the week before a wedding or a flight, however tempting the timing looks.

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Skin Temple is at Shop 8, 401 St Kilda Road, Melbourne, entered from Arnold Street, on (03) 9867 2992 or at [skintemple.com.au](http://www.skintemple.com.au/).
