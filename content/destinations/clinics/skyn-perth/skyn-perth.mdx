Hay Street in Subiaco is not short of skin clinics, but SKYN has been on this strip since 2004 and the client list has barely turned over. Catherine Lee founded it after finishing a Bachelor of Health Science in Dermal Therapies and has spent two decades building the kind of clinic that runs on referrals rather than advertising.
The menu is broad and clinical. CO2 and ND:Yag laser resurfacing, IPL, Erbium, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, PRP, PDO threads, wrinkle relaxers, dermal fillers and biostimulators. Every treatment starts with a proper consultation. Catherine is a member of the Australian Society of Dermal Clinicians and her approach leans on evidence over trend.

![SKYN treatment room with contemporary artwork](/content/destinations/clinics/skyn-perth/028a2231.jpg)

Take-home skincare is ZO Skin Health, Cosmedix and Mesoesthetic. The space itself is considered. Antonio Mora artwork on the walls, a Tom Dixon fitting in the bathroom that has become an unofficial post-treatment selfie spot. There is good coffee, Madame Flavour tea and San Pellegrino. Small touches, well chosen.

![SKYN clinic details](/content/destinations/clinics/skyn-perth/028a2227.jpg)

Laser resurfacing and rejuvenation are the most popular treatments. Wrinkle relaxers run year-round. The clinic is not flashy. It is precise, well run and quietly one of the best skin addresses in Perth.
