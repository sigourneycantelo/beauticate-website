Over ten years in the making, All Saints Skin Clinic in Double Bay is the creation of aesthetic physician to the A-list, Dr Joseph Hkeik. Situated in a designer-finished house that feels more like a palatial home than a clinic, the chic two-level space was imagined by Hkeik and brought to life by interior designer Blainey North. It features six treatment rooms, a skin consultation room complete with makeup and photography station, and even a meditation zone for clients to decompress post-treatment.

![All Saints Skin Clinic interior, Double Bay](/content/destinations/clinics/all-saints-skin-clinic-double-bay/download1.jpeg)

![Treatment area at All Saints Skin Clinic](/content/destinations/clinics/all-saints-skin-clinic-double-bay/download.jpeg)

With a client list that reads like a who's who of Australian celebrity, Hkeik is known for his holistic view of aesthetic treatments, and the concept behind his Double Bay clinic showcases that perfectly. His approach is one of restraint: creating work that is undetectable.

"Maintaining normal facial expressiveness is fundamental in my eyes. I believe that less is more. This is at the heart of my 'art of face' philosophy. You will walk out of All Saints Skin Clinic feeling and looking more beautiful, natural and radiant," says Hkeik.

![Dr Joseph Hkeik, All Saints Skin Clinic](/content/destinations/clinics/all-saints-skin-clinic-double-bay/download-1.jpeg)

The Double Bay clinic is the third All Saints practice, joining sister properties in Darlinghurst and Parramatta.
