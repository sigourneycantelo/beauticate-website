Behind an unassuming number on Castlereagh Street is a warm-taupe cocoon most of the CBD walks past without noticing. Door No.18 is a small boutique built around something rare in the city, actual wet facilities, a pool, jacuzzi, steam room and sauna you can use alongside your treatment.
The facials are customised and lean on Omnilux LED for visible results, with massage, lashes and tanning rounding out a bespoke menu. Cosmedix and Hydropeptide do the skincare heavy lifting. It is intimate by design, no couples, no crowds, just you and an afternoon off the grid. The escape hatch in the middle of the city. Keep it to yourself if you can bear to.
