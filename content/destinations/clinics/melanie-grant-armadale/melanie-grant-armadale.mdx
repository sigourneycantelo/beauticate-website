Melanie Grant is the name models and editors quietly pass between themselves, and the Melbourne studio sits in a grand townhouse on High Street Armadale to match the reputation. The palette inside is deliberately neutral, all soft stone and calm, archival fashion photography on the walls as a reminder of the world she works in. Grant made her name reading skin rather than lecturing it, and the treatments run on that instinct. High-tech facials, considered and specific, the technology used in service of the face rather than for show. No jargon thrown around to justify the price. Just the certainty that you are being read correctly, and treated accordingly. The result speaks before she does.
