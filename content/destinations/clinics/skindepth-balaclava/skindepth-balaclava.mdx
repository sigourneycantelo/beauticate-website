Most dermatology clinics are designed to make you forget you have a body. Skindepth went the other way. It sits in a refurbished early-1900s building on Balaclava Road, a checkerboard-tiled verandah out front, the kind of facade you would slow down for even without an appointment. Inside is serious cosmetic dermatology, medical-grade and specialist-led, but the setting has been given as much thought as the science. Period bones, clean contemporary treatment rooms, none of the clinical chill you brace for. It is the rare skin clinic that looks like somewhere you would choose to spend an hour. Expertise you can trust, in a building with a pulse. The verandah alone earns the visit.
