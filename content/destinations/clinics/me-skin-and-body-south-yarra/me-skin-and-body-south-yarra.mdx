## Melbourne's Quietly Iconic Skin Clinic

#### Climb the stairs of the elegant terrace on Toorak Road that houses Joey Scandizzo's salon and you'll find [Me Skin & Body](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/), a South Yarra staple of more than 25 years. It's owned by skincare expert Brooke Holmes and experienced dermal clinician Rachael Hofer, and it's the rare clinic that has both the machines and the hands.

![Signup Banner](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/signup-banner.jpg)

Brooke's philosophy is to marry skin science with an intuitive touch. In practice that means the technology is here, but nobody is in a hurry to reach for it.

![The Facial: Synergie Skindulgence in Expert Hands](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/untitled-design-4.jpg)

## The Signature Synergie Skindulgence Facial

The [signature facial](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/signature-facials/) opens with a skin analysis under a wood's lamp, then pairs [Synergie Skin's cosmeceuticals](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/synergie/) with layered massage.

A double cleanse, a Synergie lactic acid peel, then a sonophoresis infusion carrying the brand's [B serum](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/vitamin-b/) and [Hylavive](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/hyalavive/).

> *Ed's note: Synergie founder Terri Vinson is a cosmetic chemist, and [our interview with her](/interviews/creatives/terri-vinson-cosmetic-chemist/terri-vinson-cosmetic-chemist) is a good primer on what's actually in the bottles.*

![Untitled Design](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/untitled-design-1.jpg)

## Where the Hands Come In

The difference between a good facial and a great one lies in the hands. You can have every tool, laser and serum going, but a lacklustre massage doesn't hit the same.

The massage here runs long: facial muscles, the jaw and neck, then the lymphatic pathways.

![Why Massage Makes All the Difference](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/untitled-design-2.jpg)

## Buccal Massage

Then buccal massage, which is where the therapist puts gloved hands inside your mouth and works the jaw and cheek muscles from the inside. It sounds a bit nuts. It is also the reason people book this facial and not another one, and it runs about ten minutes.

Acupressure facial massage follows, at length.

![What Else Is On Offer at Me Skin & Body?](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/untitled-design-49.png)

## The Rest of the Menu

The [Cutera Excel V](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/cutera-excel-v/), which uses Laser Genesis, and the [Lutronic LaseMD Ultra](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/lasemdultra/), a fractional laser. Also microneedling, mesotherapy, LED and body treatments.

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [meskinandbody.com.au](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/) or (03) 9827 9181. Laser treatments start with a consultation.
