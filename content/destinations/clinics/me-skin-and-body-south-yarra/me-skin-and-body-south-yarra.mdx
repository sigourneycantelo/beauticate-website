## Melbourne's Quietly Iconic Skin Clinic

#### Tucked above the buzz of South Yarra’s Toorak Road sits one of Melbourne’s most quietly iconic skin clinics: [Me Skin & Body](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/)  If you climb the stairs of the elegant terrace that houses Joey Scandizzo’s salon you'll find this South Yarra staple that’s been quietly transforming skin for over 25 years. Owned by skincare expert [Brooke Holmes](https://www.facebook.com/meskinandbody/photos/a.136615019697170/6501295383229070/?type=3&m_entstream_source=timeline&__mmr=1&_rdr) and experienced dermal clinician Rachael Hofer, Me Skin & Body offers a rare blend of cutting-edge treatments and soul-soothing care.

![Signup Banner](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/signup-banner.jpg)

Brooke’s philosophy is all about marrying serious skin science with a nurturing, intuitive touch, and that’s exactly what I experienced during my visit. I was lucky enough to be booked in with co-owner Rachael, whose magic hands gave me one of the most memorable facials I’ve had in a long time.

![The Facial: Synergie Skindulgence in Expert Hands](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/untitled-design-4.jpg)


## Synergie Skindulgence Facial Treatment

### The Facial: Synergie Skindulgence in Expert Hands

The moment I lay down on the treatment bed, I knew I was in expert hands. [Rachael](https://www.instagram.com/meskinandbody/p/C83vSfYPYho/?locale=ko-KR&hl=af) started with a thorough skin analysis using a wood’s lamp, then moved into the clinic’s [Signature Synergie Skindulgence Facial](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/signature-facials/). It’s a tailored treatment that pairs [Synergie Skin’s high-performance cosmeceuticals](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/synergie/) with layered massage and gentle yet powerful actives.

![Untitled Design](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/untitled-design-1.jpg)

(As it happened, I’d just interviewed Synergie founder [Terri Vinson](/interviews/creatives/terri-vinson-cosmetic-chemist/terri-vinson-cosmetic-chemist) the day before for the podcast, so it felt serendipitous to experience the products in a treatment setting so soon after. And yes, they absolutely delivered.)

The facial began with a double cleanse and a Synergie lactic acid peel to gently exfoliate, followed by sonophoresis infusion with the brand’s [B serum](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/vitamin-b/) and [Hylavive](https://www.meskinandbody.com.au/hyalavive/) for deep, targeted hydration. But the real hero here? The massage.

![Why Massage Makes All the Difference](/content/destinations/clinics/me-skin-and-body-south-yarra/untitled-design-2.jpg)


## Why Expert Massage Transforms Your Facial

### Why Massage Makes All the Difference

I’ve always believed that the difference between a good facial and a great one lies in the hands. You can have all the tools, lasers and serums in the world, but if the massage is lacklustre, it just doesn’t hit the same. Rachael’s hands? Pure alchemy. She strummed my facial muscles, released the tension in my jaw and neck, and worked lymphatic pathways in a way that lifted, sculpted and calmed me on every level.

**What Is Buccal Massage, And Why You Need It**

Then came the pièce de résistance: buccal massage. If you haven’t tried it, it’s where the therapist uses gloved hands inside your mouth to massage your jaw and cheeks. I know, it sounds a bit nuts. But it’s unbelievably effective for releasing tension and lifting the face. When Rachael removed her hands 10 minutes later, I felt a wave of release, it was as if my jaw had unclenched for the first time in months. My face felt… spacious.


## Acupressure Bliss and Glowing Results

### Post-Facial Glow and Acupressure Bliss

She followed with a long, luxurious session of acupressure facial massage, and by the time I floated out, my skin was noticeably brighter and plumper, and I felt lighter, more grounded, and more than a little bit glowy from the inside out.

I was so impressed with how much clinical know-how Rachael brought to the treatment, but also how nurturing the whole experience was. That’s the magic of Me Skin & Body: they’re able to deliver real, skin-changing results and hold space for your nervous system to rest and reset.
