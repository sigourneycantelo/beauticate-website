Dr Naomi McCullum trained as both a cosmetic doctor and a chemist, and her Paddington flagship is as considered as her work. It lives in a 1904 Federation residence built as a church manse by the architect Charles Slatyer, tower and verandahs intact, now all dusty-pink chaises, herringbone floors and gold-foil wallpaper.
The Uptown outpost sits above Dior in a heritage building, reception styled like a boutique hotel. The menu is a set of proprietary protocols, the Manse Glow, Glass, Tight and Filter, plus Aquagold and a full device suite. The skincare is her own Dr Naomi Skin. The Manse Glow is the goal, and it usually delivers. Cosmetic medicine has rarely been this pretty.
