At one of the busiest junctions in Melbourne, in Kew, is a boutique skin clinic hidden well enough that a recognisable face could walk in unannounced and stay that way.

## Derya Koch

It's a second-floor suite, and it opens with a herbal tea and a detailed pre-consultation form. Then you meet head dermal clinician Derya Koch, who does the assessment before anyone decides on a treatment.

"I believe in prevention rather than cure, as a result, we focus on anti-ageing and perform treatments to optimise skin health and function, which should be supported by good diet and regular exercise," she says. Note to self: sort out diet and exercise.

## The Assessment

The clinic works in non-surgical cosmetic treatments across facial rejuvenation, skin concerns and leg veins, and nothing begins before a consultation. Treatments are built to the individual rather than picked off a list.

Close-up images go up on a screen, and then the filters come out. The melanin filter shows sun damage and pigmentation. The vascular filter shows facial veins and redness. Neither is a flattering way to spend a Tuesday, which is rather the point of them.

![Ultraportrait](/content/destinations/clinics/the-victorian-dermal-group-melbourne/ultraportrait-1.jpg)

## The Lactic Peel and LED

A typical prescription off that assessment is an Ultraceuticals lactic peel followed by LED light therapy.

The therapist preps the skin and paints the lactic on, talking through each stage as she goes. A mild tingling is normal and you'll be told so before it starts. Then goggles, and the LED over the face, which is like staring into the sun for the first few seconds and then like falling asleep on a beach. It finishes with an Ultraceuticals serum, a moisturiser and SPF 50+.

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [victoriandermalgroup.com.au](https://www.victoriandermalgroup.com.au/contact-us/) or (03) 9853 9264. You'll find it at Level 2, Suite 205-206, 1 Princess Street, Kew.
