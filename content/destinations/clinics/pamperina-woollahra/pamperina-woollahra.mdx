#### Off a bustling tree-lined street in Woollahra, [Pamperina](https://www.pamperina.com.au/) is a small, chic salon that does the full spread of beauty treatments in one elegantly appointed space. It's Georgie Palamountain's, and it's been treating the well-heeled of Sydney's east since it opened in early 2019.

![Dscf](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/dscf8551.jpg)

## European-Inspired Treatments

The menu runs to facials, manicures and pedicures, lash treatments and waxing, all built off the Continental ranges and techniques the therapists trained with.

![Dscf](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/dscf9075-copy.jpg)

![Dscf](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/dscf9124-copy.jpg)

## Silvia, Classical Beauty Therapist

Silvia describes herself as a classical beauty therapist, and comes with the sort of Italian warmth that makes a treatment room feel less like a treatment room. She favours non-invasive, traditional facials, and she's brought a set of European techniques with her that she's looking to introduce here.

In Italy, she says, women book a facial every month, the way French women famously do. Australian women, in her experience, are less diligent about the regular appointment.

![Pamperina, Woollahra](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/photo-2019-10-25-18-07-53.jpg)

![Dscf](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/dscf8223.jpg)

## The Signature Deep Pore Cleansing Facial

The house facial is a thorough cleanse and extractions, then an enzyme peel from Dr Spiller, then hydrating ampoules, masks and serums.

The salon works from a tight edit of brands, Dermalogica, Dr Spiller and Dr Alex, all of which you can buy on the way out.

![Dscf](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/dscf9149-2-copy.jpg)

![Pamperina](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/pamperina.png)

## The Lymphatic Drainage Massage

There's also a Lymphatic Drainage Leg and Tummy Massage, which is thorough to the point of being quite intense, and not the sort of massage you drift off during.

Silvia's own advice, and it costs nothing: ten minutes a day with your legs up a wall.

![Dscf](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/dscf9206-2-copy.jpg)

![Pamperina](/content/destinations/clinics/pamperina-woollahra/pamperina-4-1.png)

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [pamperina.com.au](https://pamperina.com.au/) or (02) 8328 0737. Book generously if you're combining a facial with the massage: together they run to about two hours.
