On Thynne Street in Bruce, Artisan runs the kind of skin clinic that reads more like a quiet gallery than a doctor's rooms. Green tones, plenty of plants, nothing shouting. The treatment list is where the seriousness shows.
Clear and Brilliant, Fraxel Dual, Laser Genesis and HydraFacial sit alongside skin needling, so this is results-led work rather than a pamper. It is part of a national group with a real medical backbone. Late Tuesdays until eight help if your skin only gets a look-in after work. Come for the lasers. Leave looking like you slept for a week.
