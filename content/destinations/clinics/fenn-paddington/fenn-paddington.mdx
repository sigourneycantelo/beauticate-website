Nicole Manning and Clare McColl are two of the most respected skin therapists in the country, and FENN is where they practise their particular brand of restraint. Akin Atelier designed the Paddington space and the design world took note.

![FENN, Paddington, glass brick and marble skin studio interior](/content/destinations/clinics/fenn-paddington/hero.jpg)

Glass bricks scatter the daylight, an apricot basin sits on Dolce Vita marble, and the rooms deepen from pale calm to earthy warmth as you move through them. The approach is high-performance but never preachy, results-led facials built on a carefully curated shelf, Rationale, Medik8, Omnilux, LESSE, Josh Rosebrook. Book the FENN Nurture Treatment or the LESSE ritual. Skin health with a real point of view, and a room to match it.
