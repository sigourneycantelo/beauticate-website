*Formerly Realskin Clinic, Hamilton.*

The Facial Hub sits in Racecourse Village in Ascot, minutes from the Brisbane River, in a contemporary space with enough glass to feel quintessentially Queensland. Bright, sunny, fresh. The parallels with the carefree nature of the sunny state stop at the door, because this is not a clinic that is relaxed about skin.

## Sevine Forster

The owner is Sevine Forster, who has a degree in Biomedical Science, trained at the London School of Beauty, and has spent more than twenty years in skin. Beauty and brains, which is a rarer combination in this industry than it should be.

She ran the business as Realskin Clinic in Hamilton for years before the move to Ascot and the change of name.

## What's on the Menu

Sculpting buccal facials, the intraoral technique where the therapist works the jaw and cheek muscles from inside the mouth. Spicule herbal peels. HydraFacial. SkinPen skin needling. IPL, LED light therapy and microdermabrasion. Spray tanning and cryotherapy round it out.

## The Wellness Hub

There's also a sauna and a cold plunge on site, which is what turns a facial appointment into a longer morning if you want it to be.

![The Facial Hub product display and treatment products](/content/destinations/clinics/realskin-clinic-hamilton/psx_20170526_131028-1.jpg)

## Booking and Hours

Open Monday and Tuesday 9am to 5pm, Wednesday and Thursday 9am to 8pm, Friday 9am to 5pm and Saturday 9am to 4pm. Those two late nights are the useful detail here.

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [The Facial Hub's booking page](https://bookings.gettimely.com/thefacialhub/bb/book) or (07) 3268 5488, and you'll find it at 188 Nudgee Road, Ascot.
