The name used to be SurFACEskin, which told you exactly where its attention stopped. SURSKIN dropped the FACE to make a point. The work here goes past the surface.
On Cambridge Street in West Leederville the menu runs from injectables and dermal therapies to lymphatic drainage and something called the Crystal Energy Facial, which is more restful than the name suggests. There is a private sauna and a cold plunge on site, so skin and body get treated in the same visit. The Wellness Ritual for Two puts a couple through all of it in an afternoon. It is skin care that behaves like a spa day. Book the ritual. Bring someone.
