Founded in 1987 by Benni Tognini — a three-time Australian Hairdresser of the Year, five-time Educator of the Year and World Master Hairdresser — Tognini's is one of Brisbane's most storied salons and a genuine institution in Australian hairdressing.

Now based in a purpose-built space in Newstead, the family-run salon is led by the next generation of Togninis alongside a team of award-winning stylists and Wella Colour Experts renowned for precision cutting, bespoke colour, blonding, balayage and seamless extensions. At the 2026 Australian Hair Industry Awards, Tognini's was named Australia's most nominated salon, taking home three wins. Nearly four decades on, the salon remains a creative powerhouse where mentorship, artistry and individuality sit at the heart of every appointment.
