#### On a tree-lined street in Melbourne's bayside Brighton, [Skin Brilliance](http://skinbrilliancebrighton.com.au/) is a boutique clinic that would rather be called an institute of skin science. Do not arrive expecting sweet scents, herbal teas and a fluffy pamper-packed afternoon. The women here mean serious business, and they say so at the door.

## Who's Doing the Treatment

You're met by a Senior Paramedical Skin Therapist. Helena holds a Bachelor of Health Science in Dermal Therapies and is a walking encyclopaedia on the subject, which is the sort of thing you want to establish before anyone points a light at your face.

The clinic's stated focus is paramedical work: Intense Pulsed Light, chemical peels, skin needling, microdermabrasion and medical devices, alongside clinical medifacials and injectables.

![Skin Brilliance, Brighton](/content/destinations/clinics/skin-brilliance-brighton/p1-1-1.jpg)

## The Consult Comes First

Every first visit starts with a three-page consultation form. It asks about your medical history and your day and night skincare routine, and then, with no particular delicacy, about your wine consumption.

Then the Skin Diagnostic Treatment: your skin assessed under an ultraviolet magnifying lamp, which looks at the epidermis on the surface and the dermis underneath it, and photographs both. Damage in the dermis is the harder of the two to work on. The lamp is not flattering and isn't meant to be. A treatment and a homecare regime get recommended off the back of it.

## The Mesoéclat Treatment

The *mesoéclat* Intensive Anti-Ageing Treatment, by cosmeceuticals company mesoestetic, is one of the most-booked things here.

It opens with a hydra milk cleanse and a hydratonic tone, then an exfoliation using glycolic ampoules. The mesoestetic radiance DNA serum is massaged in, and a crystal fibre mask from the same range goes over the top, with a hand and arm massage while it sits. It finishes with the radiance DNA intensive cream and an SPF.

The clinic positions it for women in their forties and beyond, and also runs it after chemical peels or microdermabrasion.

![Skin Brilliance, Brighton](/content/destinations/clinics/skin-brilliance-brighton/p2-1-1.jpg)

## Booking

Bookings go through [skinbrilliancebrighton.com.au](http://skinbrilliancebrighton.com.au/) or (03) 9592 4345, and the clinic is at 123 Were Street, Brighton. Injectables start with a consultation.

Don't go expecting to be pampered.
