Shop 2, 171 Clarence Street is a short walk from both Town Hall and Wynyard, which makes this one dangerously convenient for anyone who works in the CBD. The French-inspired interior is blonde wood floors, crisp white walls and a considered fit-out that manages to be welcoming without going cold and clinical. Sea foam linen chairs in reception, and a consultation form to fill out in one.

## The Backstory

Clear Skincare was founded in 1995 by Dr Philippa McCaffery, who worked in women's health and adult acne and was among the first in Australia to use cosmetic lasers. She built a single clinic into a national network.

In 2018 the business was acquired by Australian Pharmaceutical Industries for $127.4 million. Wesfarmers then acquired API in 2022, folding Clear Skincare into Wesfarmers Health alongside Priceline Pharmacy. That backing is what took it past 90 clinics across Australia and New Zealand.

Whether that appeals depends on what you want from a clinic. What corporate ownership buys here is consistency: Melbourne, Brisbane or Clarence Street, you get the same fit-out, the same equipment and the same training behind the person holding the device.

## What's on the Menu

The offering has widened a long way from the laser-only days. HydraFacial, the multi-step facial that cleanses, extracts and hydrates in one sitting. MediPeels, the clinic's clinical-grade chemical peels. LED light therapy, microdermabrasion and microneedling. Sylfirm RF needling, which pairs radiofrequency with needling. The Signature Acne treatments, built on Dr McCaffery's original protocols. Laser hair removal, still the brand's best known service. And cosmetic injectables, administered by qualified practitioners.

## What Laser Hair Removal Involves

You're asked to shave the area beforehand. In the room, protective glasses go on, the area is cleaned and marked up with a highlighter pen, and the clinician works with a device that has a cooling tip built in, plus a second cooling device that chills the skin further.

An underarm appointment is done in about ten minutes. A cooling gel goes on at the end and you leave with an aftercare pack: a post-laser gel, an exfoliating cloth and a microdermabrasion cream with glycolic acid. Laser is a course of treatments rather than a single visit, so ask what a full course involves at consultation.
