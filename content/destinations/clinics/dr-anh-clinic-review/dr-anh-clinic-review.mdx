[Dr Anh](http://www.dranh.com.au/) is in the middle of Perth's CBD and does its best not to look like it. The clinic occupies Level 1 of a heritage building on William Street with almost no signage on the street, and you go up by private elevator. If you've ever stood in a ground-floor clinic reception hoping not to run into anyone, you'll understand the design brief immediately.

## Privacy as the Floor Plan

The privacy isn't only the entrance. There are private consultation rooms rather than a shared consult space, lockers and bathrooms for clients, and the payment desk sits at the back of the clinic, away from the main reception, so nobody settles an account in front of a waiting room.

Dr Anh's stated position is that client comfort comes first here. The floor plan is the part of that you can actually verify.

## Dr Anh Nguyen

Dr Anh Nguyen is a plastic surgeon and the clinic's owner. She opened it in October 2014, after more than a decade in cosmetic surgery, with the intention of building a private setting for less-invasive cosmetic work. She works with a team of nurses, dermal therapists and beauty therapists.

## Inside the Clinic

The waiting room runs to tufted wing back chairs, gilded tables and chandeliers, with chilled water and quiet music, and inspirational quotes on the walls, which will either lift you or won't.

Behind it: treatment rooms for facials and massage, private consultation rooms, a Body Boost Bed, a makeup application room and a surgery theatre.

## What's on the Menu

Cosmetic procedures, surgical and non-surgical, alongside wellness and medispa treatments. The Mesoeclat medifacial is one of the deeply exfoliating options on the skin side.

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [dranh.com.au](https://dranh.com.au/contact-us/) or (08) 9322 2659. Surgical and injectable treatments start with a consultation.
