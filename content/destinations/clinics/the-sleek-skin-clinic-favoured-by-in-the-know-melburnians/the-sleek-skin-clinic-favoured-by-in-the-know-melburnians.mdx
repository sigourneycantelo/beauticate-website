#### Tucked behind tall hedges on Toorak Road, in that most old money of Melbourne suburbs, [Liberty Belle](https://www.libertybelle.com.au/skin-centre/) is a purpose-built compound devoted to aesthetics. Twin buildings in pale stone, well tended jasmine, and a choice to make on arrival. The plastic surgery centre under Dr Chris Moss is on the right. The skin clinic is on the left, for those who want something done without going under anything.

![Liberty Belle](/content/destinations/clinics/the-sleek-skin-clinic-favoured-by-in-the-know-melburnians/liberty-belle-1-1.jpg)

## The Assessment Comes First

Nothing starts until a therapist has looked at your skin properly. A thorough double cleanse under bright lights, then an assessment made by the person doing the treatment rather than taken on trust from the client's own account of it.

It should be the standard way every treatment begins, and it isn't. Plenty of clinics open with whale song and an aromatherapy oil selection instead.

![Liberty Belle](/content/destinations/clinics/the-sleek-skin-clinic-favoured-by-in-the-know-melburnians/liberty-belle-2-1.jpg)

## Then the Photographs

You're taken to a separate room and photographed bare-faced from every angle. It's a slightly exposing ten minutes and the clinic knows it, which is why they say up front that the images stay with clinic staff.

![Jo](/content/destinations/clinics/the-sleek-skin-clinic-favoured-by-in-the-know-melburnians/jo1_7046.jpg)

## The Dermasweep Facial

Liberty Belle's Dermasweep is gentler than standard dermabrasion. The tool has two heads and three jobs: a tiny street sweeper head that brushes off debris, a suction head that lifts it away from the face, and a smooth cooling element that puts serum back into the cleaned skin. The treatment is built to the skin in front of it, which matters if you've arrived with a compromised barrier from a prescription retinoid.

![Liberty Belle](/content/destinations/clinics/the-sleek-skin-clinic-favoured-by-in-the-know-melburnians/liberty-belle-3.jpg)

## Extractions and Yellow LED

Manual extractions follow, and this is where a good therapist shows herself: active spots that aren't ready to come to the surface get left alone, because manipulating them does more harm than leaving them. A balm for sensitive skin goes on, then twenty minutes under the yellow LED, then a second serum and a moisturiser with SPF.

![Liberty Belle RX Superhero Antioxidant Moisturiser](/content/destinations/clinics/the-sleek-skin-clinic-favoured-by-in-the-know-melburnians/liberty-belle-4.jpg)

###### Liberty Belle RX Superhero Antioxidant Moisturiser

A note for fellow claustrophobes. The LED runs three panels, one directly overhead and one at each side, and it isn't close or confining. Banish any thought of an MRI. Therapists ask outright how you feel about it, show you the machine first, and stay in the room the whole time whether you're nervous or not.

![Liberty Belle](/content/destinations/clinics/the-sleek-skin-clinic-favoured-by-in-the-know-melburnians/liberty-belle-5-1.jpg)

## The Waiting Room

The space is scented with fresh flowers and the treatment rooms are named after Coco and Marilyn, which tells you where the clinic thinks it sits.

It also shares a waiting room with the surgery side, and that produces the occasional tableau. On one afternoon, a woman under bandages being quietly tended to, a staff member murmuring that the car was arriving now, and a gleaming black limousine at the kerb with a driver in a smart suit and a Secret Service earpiece. The Uber X waiting behind it did not compete.
