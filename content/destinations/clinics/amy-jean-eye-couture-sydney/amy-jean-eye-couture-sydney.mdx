Stepping into the St James Trust Building, you know you're about to have a luxurious experience. And Amy Jean's salon -- now on Level 10, having outgrown its original sixth-floor digs -- doesn't disappoint. Once known as Amy Jean Eye Couture, the business has since rebranded to Amy Jean Brows, a name that better reflects the empire it has become. Regarded as the "Arch Angel" to Australia's A-list, AJ has built a reputation as being the best at her business -- and that is the business of "dressing the eyes".

![Amy Jean Brows lash and brow results](/content/destinations/clinics/amy-jean-eye-couture-sydney/lash-brow-results.jpg)

###### Signature Brow and Lash Results at Amy Jean Brows

Just as a couturier creates bespoke garments for his clientele, Amy Jean analyses her client's face and creates stunning lashes and brows that perfectly frame the eyes -- which she has done for everyone from Dannii Minogue to Delta Goodrem. What began as a single suite above Sydney's Bambini Trust restaurant has expanded into five salons nationally: Sydney CBD, Double Bay, Melbourne's Armadale, Brisbane's Fortitude Valley, and the Gold Coast. There have even been pop-up salons in Perth and Paris.

"I believe eyes are windows to the soul," says Amy Jean. "I love to create and I've always been a keen artist." Although it can take months to secure an appointment with the brow queen herself, AJ has trained her staff in her particularly precise technique and she closely coaches them to maintain a top level of service.

The service menu has evolved considerably since our first visit. Feathertouch brow tattooing has been superseded by Amy Jean's signature Mist Brow Tattoo -- a softer, more natural-looking technique that builds buildable density. The lash lift has also had an upgrade: the Korean Lash Lift delivers a wider-eyed, more lifted result that lasts around eight weeks. Keratin Brow Lamination smooths unruly arches into a brushed-up, editorial shape. For those wanting something more permanent, there is Kiss Me Lip Tint tattoo, Forever Lash Liner, and areola tattooing -- services that speak to the clinic's expanding scope beyond brows alone. LED light therapy and Pico laser removal round out the treatment card.

![Amy Jean Privee Collection brow products](/content/destinations/clinics/amy-jean-eye-couture-sydney/privee-collection.jpg)

###### The Privee Collection -- Amy Jean's Own Brow and Lash Range

Amy Jean has also channelled her expertise into a product line, the Privee Collection, which is now stocked at Sephora and Adore Beauty as well as in-salon. Every service at Amy Jean Brows is finished with a touch-up using these products, so clients leave looking polished rather than freshly waxed.
