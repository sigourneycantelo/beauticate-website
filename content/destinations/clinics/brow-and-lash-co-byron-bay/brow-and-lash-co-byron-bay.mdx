Byron runs on a certain kind of low effort that takes real effort. Brow & Lash Co is where a lot of it happens. They call themselves a beach to bar beauty destination, which is about right. Brows first. Waxed, tinted, laminated into that fluffed-up shape everyone pretends they woke up with.

![Brow and Lash Co, Byron Bay](/content/destinations/clinics/brow-and-lash-co-byron-bay/hero.jpg)

Then lashes, classic through to Russian volume, or a set of silks if you want it softer. Finish with a spray tan mixed to your depth so you never go orange. It is on Byron Street, minutes from the sand. Walk in pale and blank. Walk out holiday-ready.
