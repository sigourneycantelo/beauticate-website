## Inside Academy Face and Body

Academy Face and Body sits in an office building set back from the cafés and shops of Subiaco, which is the first thing it offers: nobody sees you go in. It's led by cosmetic surgeon Dr Jayson Oates, and the menu runs the full width, from LED and needling through laser and dermaplaning to surgery.

## The Room

Reception is sleek and modern, minimal furniture, neutral tones, fresh flowers. Along one wall, Ultraceuticals and UltraMD skincare and mineral makeup, displayed temptingly enough to do damage on the way out.

What's more striking is the greeting. Not just the reception team but every person who crosses the waiting room, and none of it appears to be policy.

![Academy Portrait](/content/destinations/clinics/academy-face-and-body-subiaco-wa/academy3portrait.jpeg)

## Claire Ross and the Skin Side

Dermal therapist Claire Ross is a proper skin nerd, which is the correct kind of person to be tucked into a treatment bed by. She asks about your skin, cleanses it, then examines it under a light before deciding anything.

A typical treatment from there: an Ultra Lactic Acid 30% peel to break up congestion and lift dead skin cells, a mild tingle rather than anything unpleasant, helped along by a skin cooling machine. Then a vitamin serum worked in with a sonophoresis machine. Then twenty minutes under the LED. Sunscreen at the end, massaged rather than smeared.

## The Clinic's Own Argument

"A huge part of any successful treatment outcome is a patient who feels listened to and really well cared for," Claire says. "It's about more than just the treatments, there's a real passion here that means we don't just perform a treatment and then say good-bye. We provide information and support every step of the way so that our patients feel their concerns are really being heard and addressed."

Less sales spin than most clinics manage, and you leave with an at-home routine rather than just a receipt.

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [academyfaceandbody.com.au](https://academyfaceandbody.com.au/) or (08) 9382 4800. You'll find it at Suite 1a, Arcadia Chambers, 1 Roydhouse Street, Subiaco. Surgical, laser and injectable treatments start with a consultation.
