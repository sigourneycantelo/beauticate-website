## A Skin Clinic on Fitzgerald Street

*La Belle Peau* sits on bustling Fitzgerald Street in North Perth, in a lovingly restored colonial property. High ceilings, polished floorboards, plush towels. It looks like a traditional beauty salon and doesn't behave like one.

## Kim Tran

The owner is Kim Tran, known locally as Perth's skin whisperer. She came to paramedical skin care the way a lot of the good ones do, by way of her own severe acne as a teenager, and opened La Belle Peau in 2007. She works with a team of internationally qualified dermal therapists, beauty therapists and cosmetic physicians.

## The Extreme Skin Work Out

ESWO is Kim's own, performed only here and only by her, and some clients fly into Perth for it. It stacks five customised paramedical strength treatments into one sitting, which is why it runs to about three hours.

It opens with a skin consultation and VISIA analysis, which is what determines the rest: sun damage, congestion and fine lines get identified and targeted rather than treated generically. Then a double cleanse of the face and *décolletage*, then diamond microdermabrasion to take off surface dead skin cells.

## The Peel, the Light and the Needling

Next a customised peel to decongest the pores, then Cryobroadlight, a light therapy treatment that does sting a little.

Numbing cream goes on and sits for a few minutes before the direct needle therapy, which is the part everyone braces for. Under the numbing cream it reads mostly as pressure.

Three hours is a long time to have someone working on your face, so it matters that Kim talks through each stage as she goes.

## The Downtime, Which Is Real

ESWO has a recovery period and it's worth planning around rather than discovering. Expect redness for the first two days, a possible minor breakout around days two and three, and peeling around the nose, chin and cheeks from days two to four.

Which makes this a treatment to book with a clear diary behind it, not the week before a wedding.
