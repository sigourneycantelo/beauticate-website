Vaia Beauty runs skin and wellness under one lofty terraced roof in East Sydney: facials and body treatments, infrared sauna, herbal and wellness consultations, and the ordinary beauty maintenance most people are actually booking for. It belongs to Vaia Pappas, a herbalist and skin therapist twenty years into the industry.

## From a Single Room in Surry Hills

Vaia spent more than a decade working out of a tiny single-treatment-room salon in Surry Hills before this. The move into a properly interior-designed building, marble bench tops, earthy finishes and her own artworks on the walls, has clearly been a labour of love. There are plans to put a crèche in, so parents can book something for themselves without arranging the rest of the day around it.

## The Facial Room

The facial room is upstairs and feels less like a clinic than like being let into someone's house. Bunches of fresh spring blooms, crystals arranged in clusters, a writing desk looking out to a balcony heaving with potted plants, and a small bookcase packed with health and beauty tomes.

![Facial treatment room at Vaia Beauty with fresh flowers and crystals](/content/destinations/clinics/vaia-beauty-darlinghurst/DermaluxVAIA047copy3.jpg)

## The Ultraceuticals Signature Facial

The house treatment is the Ultraceuticals Signature Facial, with LED light as an add-on. What people come back for is the massage: cleansing and facial work with a very practised set of strokes, carried down into the neck and shoulders, then a mask, then hands and feet done with the Ultraceuticals Body Retexturising Complex.

"The goal is pure relaxation today," Vaia says of her approach. "But I guess it's just about really understanding and hearing a client's needs, understanding and guiding them."

On stocking Ultraceuticals alongside her own range: "I love that they use high-grade cosmetic ingredients and create potent formulations."

![Vaia Pappas preparing skincare products at the clinic](/content/destinations/clinics/vaia-beauty-darlinghurst/DermaluxVAIA006copy.jpg)

## The Rest of the Menu

Waxing, tinting, nails, spray tans and infrared sauna sessions, plus herbal and wellness consultations, with formulas Vaia hand-mixes on site.

The useful thing about the combination is the diary: a consultation and your beauty maintenance in the same visit rather than two.

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through (02) 9380 7588 or [@vaiabeauty](https://www.instagram.com/vaiabeauty/) on Instagram, and the clinic is at 32B Burton Street, Darlinghurst.

One last thing that tells you the register of the place: she offered to repaint a chipped manicure while the LED did its work, then had to help its owner back into her coat, the polish still being wet.
