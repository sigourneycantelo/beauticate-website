Chanel Corban has been perfecting the art of the spray tan for fifteen years, and Glow Haus is the grown-up home she built for it. Gray and Co designed the Potts Point studio in beige, cream, walnut and bronze, with travertine surfaces and heavy linen curtains dividing the zones, professional without a hint of clinical.
The tan is the hero, custom-mixed with a choice of developers, airbrushed to whatever depth you dare. Beyond it there are laminated brows, lash lifts and an infrared sauna out the back. Grounded, refined and quietly glamorous. Proof that a fake tan can be a luxury service, in the right hands and the right light.
