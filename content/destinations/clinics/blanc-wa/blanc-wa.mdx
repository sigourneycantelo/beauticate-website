## A Discreet Cottesloe Cosmetic Clinic

#### Napoleon Street is one block back from the boutiques that make Cottesloe Cottesloe, which is exactly the point. [Blanc](https://www.blancskin.com.au/) is the sort of clinic you have to know about.

The fit-out is by interior architect Sarah Coghlan of Within, in pale grey and pink, with furniture from James Said and [Mobilia](https://www.mobilia.com.au/). The walls carry ethereal paintings by local artist Francesca Gnagnarella. The flowers are by [Natural Art Flowers](https://www.naturalartflowers.com.au/), the handwash in the treatment rooms is Aesop, and the clinic has its own fragrance, blended a few streets away by clean beauty shop [Heartwood](https://heartwoodco.com.au/). None of it announces itself. That's the trick.

![Blanc, Perth](/content/destinations/clinics/blanc-wa/img_1022.jpg)

## Dr Clara Hurst: Harley Street Expertise

Blanc belongs to [Dr Clara Hurst](/interviews/founders/the-glamorous-cosmetic-injector-youll-want-to-fly-to-perth-to-see/the-glamorous-cosmetic-injector-youll-want-to-fly-to-perth-to-see), who was educated at King's College London Medical and Dental School and worked as a cosmetic injector on Harley Street, London's most sought-after address for anything medical, before moving to Perth and opening her own doors. Her interview with us is worth reading for the Harley Street years alone.

![Blanc, Perth](/content/destinations/clinics/blanc-wa/img_1408.jpg)

## The Signature Facial

The Signature Facial runs 90 minutes and starts with a skin assessment, which determines the cleansers and the peel used on the day rather than the other way around. From there it takes in facial steaming, electrical muscle stimulation, a 3D nano crystal mask and time under an LED light. The massage isn't confined to the face. It carries on through the *décolleté*, the arms and the legs, which is more hands-on than a clinic facial usually bothers with.

![Blanc, Perth](/content/destinations/clinics/blanc-wa/img_1100.jpg)

## The Rest of the Menu

Beyond facials, Blanc offers cosmetic injectables, laser and peel treatments, waxing and eyelash tinting. It's also the only clinic in Western Australia where the Accor Plasma Pen is available.

![Blanc, Perth](/content/destinations/clinics/blanc-wa/img_1274.jpg)

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [blancskin.com.au](https://blancskin.com.au/) or (08) 9284 5225. You'll find it at 8/12 Napoleon Street, a short walk up from the beach and a world away from it.
