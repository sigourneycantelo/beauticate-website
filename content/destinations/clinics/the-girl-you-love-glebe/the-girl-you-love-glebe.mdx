## Behind the Peony-Pink Door

The room is light and bright, the tea is T2 Jade Mountain Green, and French sixties pop is playing. There's a vintage settee, a butterfly screen, floral wallpaper, and some serious-looking skincare on the stands by the door. It is a very small operation and none of it is accidental.

![The Girl You Love salon waiting area with vintage settee and butterfly screen](/content/destinations/clinics/the-girl-you-love-glebe/L1-3.jpg)

## Kay

Kay has been in the industry 36 years. She worked across Sydney's eastern suburbs and inner west, then spent a few years in spas on cruise ships sailing around the world, then came back to a posting as area manager for a large chain of medispas.

Then she gave it up, which is the interesting part.

"My passion was for skin, not for managing staff," she says. "I wanted to get back to the art of facials and caring for my clients."

![Skincare products on display at The Girl You Love](/content/destinations/clinics/the-girl-you-love-glebe/P2-5.jpg)

## What She Works With

Signature facials built on active professional ranges: Elizabeth Arden Pro, Medik8, Priori and Hydropeptide. Skin needling and peels sit alongside them. She also has a background in makeup artistry and does makeup application in the salon, which is a rarer combination than it sounds.

![Floral-wallpapered salon interior with vintage furniture and décor](/content/destinations/clinics/the-girl-you-love-glebe/L2-3.jpg)

## The Hydropeptide Facial

A double cleanse, then an exfoliation using vitamin C, microdermabrasion crystals and a gentle AHA gel, each phase massaged on rather than applied and taken off with a warm towel. Then the steam, and a long knead through the hands and arms while you're under it.

Extractions come next, fingers wrapped in tissue, with proper attention paid to the blackheads around the nose. Then a toner, an eye cream and a serum.

The facial massage uses a coconut calming balm, and she strums the face like a guitar, fingers beating rhythmically across it. An enzyme *soufflé* mask goes on, and while it sets she spends a decent stretch on the neck and shoulders, which is always the bonus round in a facial.

It finishes with Power Serum, the Face Lift lightweight day moisturiser and Solar Defense, a tinted sunscreen, so you can leave and go straight somewhere.
