Korean nail art is a discipline, not a manicure, and eclaire treats it accordingly. The CBD studio is done out like a small gallery, all clean white and cool chrome, the kind of pared-back setting that makes whatever is happening at the table look like the exhibit. Because here it is. This is where you go for the intricate, hand-painted, faintly obsessive nail work that Seoul does better than anywhere and Melbourne has been slow to match. Every set is bespoke, built freehand, closer to jewellery than polish. White walls, chrome fittings, ten tiny canvases. Come with a reference and leave with something no one else has. Precision as an art form.
