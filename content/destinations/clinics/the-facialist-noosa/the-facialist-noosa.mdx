Anna Walker trained under some of the best facialists in the world and now works out of a small private studio at Noosa Heads. The Facialist is pared back on purpose. No noise, no upsell, just skin. Walker specialises in face sculpting and buccal massage, the inside-the-cheek work that lifts and drains, and she is Reiki trained on top of it.
Skincare is healthcare is her line, and she means it. The shelves are Sodashi and Murad, botanical and clinical in equal measure. New clients get a proper consult first. Book the sculpting facial and watch your jaw reappear. She knows exactly what she is doing.
