Maryann has been doing skin for thirty-four years, which means she has watched every trend arrive, peak and quietly disappear. Skin & Tonic, her room on Rusden Street, is built against all that. No chasing the new thing. Just the long game of skin that holds up over decades. She works with bio-microneedling and skin needling, has an infrared sauna, and stocks the good, considered lines. The Beauty Chef, Meder, Inika Organic. Armidale is not where you expect this level of skin thinking. That is precisely why it is worth the drive up. Experience you cannot fake. Only earn.
