Habitat is the industrial precinct where Byron keeps its good coffee and its architects. Aesthetica sits inside it, which tells you something before you walk in. The room is calm rather than clinical, run by medical and dermal people who clearly did not want it to feel like a hospital.
The menu is proper. Injectables, skin needling, LED, PRP, plasma lift and a skin nutrition consult if you want to fix things from the inside. It is locally owned, cruelty-free and signed up to Sustainable Salons. Results without the sterile chill. That is a harder balance than it looks.
