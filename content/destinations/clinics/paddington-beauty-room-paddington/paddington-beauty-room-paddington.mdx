In an elegant monochrome terrace beside the Five Ways intersection in Paddington sits a salon that has been there over 14 years, which in that stretch of Glenmore Road counts as an institution.

![The Salon Has a Warm, Inviting Feel](/content/destinations/clinics/paddington-beauty-room-paddington/salon-2.jpeg)

###### The Salon Has a Warm, Inviting Feel

## Sleek Interiors and Full-Service Treatments

High ceilings, chandeliers, warm polished floorboards and silver accents picked to match the La Prairie and Dermalogica on display. Four treatment rooms and a spray tan room, which means several things can happen at once: facials, waxing, IPL, St Tropez spray tans, manicures, brows. Built, in other words, for the time-poor.

## Anna Field

Anna Field has been in the industry 28 years, and spent ten of them as National Training and Promotions Manager for La Prairie. That job meant travelling the world teaching therapists how to give the facial, and learning the products from the people running research and development.

What you get from that, besides the massage, is no-nonsense advice delivered mid-treatment. She is not precious about telling you what to stop doing.

![Anna Relishes Giving Advice to Her Clients](/content/destinations/clinics/paddington-beauty-room-paddington/giving.jpeg)

###### Anna Relishes Giving Advice to Her Clients

If you can't get in with Anna, the floor is not a drop-off. Every therapist has at least five years behind them and is trained on an ongoing basis by Anna herself.

![The Ultimate One Stop Shop for Every Beauty Need](/content/destinations/clinics/paddington-beauty-room-paddington/need.jpeg)

###### A One Stop Shop for Every Beauty Need

## The Caviar Executive La Prairie Facial

The Caviar Executive is the 90-minute La Prairie facial condensed to a lunch hour, keeping the signature massage, which is the part worth keeping. Book a manicure alongside it and a second therapist works on your hands while the facial runs, which is either extremely efficient or slightly decadent depending on the day you're having.

## Top to Toe

The salon also has an arrangement with neighbouring hair salon Wilson Marz for a blow dry after your treatment, so you can leave with clean hair as well. Ask when booking, since the arrangement and its price aren't ones we can confirm from here.

## Booking and Prices

Prices vary by treatment and aren't listed here. Bookings and current pricing go through [the salon's booking page](https://thepaddingtonbeautyroom.mylocalsalon.com/onlinebooking/v7410/Steps/SiteSelection.aspx) or (02) 9356 8700, and you'll find it at 212 Glenmore Road, Paddington.
