**[Rêve Skin + Beauty](https://www.reveskinandbeauty.com.au/)** holds a corner position on Booth and View Street in Annandale, and announces itself quietly: a warm burgundy exterior, rich and elegant, and at Christmas an oversized bow on the front door.

![2026 website feature images](/content/destinations/clinics/reve-skin-beauty-annandale/2026-website-feature-images.gif)

## Women-Only, By Design

The clinic is women-only, which was **[Meghann Nikola's](https://www.reveskinandbeauty.com.au/about)** decision when she founded it and remains the organising principle of the place.

Her background is in science and pharmaceutical marketing, and it shows in how treatments get chosen here. The pitch isn't indulgence. It's evidence first, delivered without the intimidation that clinical spaces often carry with them.

<PullQuote>A high-end experience, without the intimidation that sometimes accompanies clinical spaces.</PullQuote>

### Thoughtful Design, Inside and Out

![Rêve Skin + Beauty Annandale treatment room](/content/destinations/clinics/reve-skin-beauty-annandale/14-scaled.png)

Soft lighting that flatters rather than interrogates. Cabinetry that's as practical as it is good-looking. And charging cables built into the treatment beds, because somebody here has actually lain on one for an hour with a flat phone.

### What's on the Menu

![sydney anti wrinkle treatments](/content/destinations/clinics/reve-skin-beauty-annandale/sydney-anti-wrinkle-treatments.jpg)

**[Lash lifts and tints](https://www.reveskinandbeauty.com.au/lash-lift)**, the **[Hydrafacial](https://www.reveskinandbeauty.com.au/skin/hydrafacial-sydney)**, which uses glycolic and salicylic acids, and **[manicures](https://www.reveskinandbeauty.com.au/nail-treatments-sydney)** downstairs. Skin treatments finish with **[Synergie Skincare](/wellness/mindset/i-thought-i-wasnt-enough-terri-vinson-jones-on-body-image-anxiety-and-self-worth/i-thought-i-wasnt-enough-terri-vinson-jones-on-body-image-anxiety-and-self-worth)** and a generous layer of **[UberZinc](/sigourneys-edit/edit/winter-beauty-rituals/winter-beauty-rituals)**. The full list is on the **[service menu](https://www.reveskinandbeauty.com.au/service-menu)**.

Because the treatments sit across two floors, they can be stacked in a single visit. When one hands over to the next, the therapists hand over too, so the aftercare from the first treatment gets explained before the second one starts. It's a small piece of choreography and most clinics don't bother with it.

![Rêve Skin + Beauty Annandale treatment room](/content/destinations/clinics/reve-skin-beauty-annandale/9-scaled.png)

### The Team

Faireen has more than fifteen years in the beauty industry. Samantha handles lashes, Sara does nails. What they have in common is the particular touch that only comes from years of doing it, the kind that tells you within thirty seconds whether you can relax.
