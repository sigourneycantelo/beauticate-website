Everything at Reju is glass and light. The rooms are walled in it, the tables are made of it, and the effect is meant to leave you feeling grounded rather than clinical. Tucked into Lonsdale Street, it gathers the whole modern recovery arsenal in one place.

![Reju, Braddon](/content/destinations/wellness/reju-braddon/hero.jpg)

Cryotherapy, hyperbaric oxygen, infrared saunas, IV infusion, compression, remedial massage. It opened as one of the more serious recovery clinics in the country, and the minimalism is the point. Nothing here distracts from the work. You come in tired. You leave with your edges sanded off. Longevity, done without a single scented candle in sight.
