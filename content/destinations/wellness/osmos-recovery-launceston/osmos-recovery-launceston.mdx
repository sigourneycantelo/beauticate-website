Each room is yours alone. OSMOS in Launceston skipped the communal model entirely and built private suites, every one with its own infrared sauna, ice bath and shower, so the only body heat in the room is yours.

![OSMOS Recovery Spaces, Launceston](/content/destinations/wellness/osmos-recovery-launceston/hero.jpg)

You get sixty minutes and no instructions. Move from heat to cold and back in whatever order feels right, on Glen Dhu Street, with the door shut on the rest of the day. Bring up to two others if solitude is not the goal. The design is quiet and considered, the kind of space that does not shout about being good. Reset, without an audience.
