On Progress Drive in Nightcliff, Encore has quietly become Darwin's serious reformer address. Real Pilates on reformer and mat, taught properly, not the choreography-and-good-luck version.

![Encore Pilates and Wellness, Nightcliff](/content/destinations/wellness/encore-pilates-nightcliff/hero.jpg)

What sets it apart is what comes after the class. Contrast therapy for the recovery circuit and mild hyperbaric oxygen, which delivers oxygen under pressure to speed the body's own repair. So the studio works as a training room and a recovery room in one. You can push hard on the reformer, then let the chamber do the rest. Strength and repair under one roof. Turns out Darwin was ready for it.
