Most reformer studios feel like a dentist waiting room. in:HAUS does not. It is open-plan and full of natural light, the kind of room you actually want to linger in after class. The full kit is here. Reformer, Trapeze table, Wunda Chair, mat. Colour shifts through the space as you move, and there is a chromotherapy panel glowing away in the two-person infrared sauna out back.

![in:HAUS Wellness, Byron Bay](/content/destinations/wellness/inhaus-wellness-byron-bay/hero.jpg)

Barre, yoga, sound healing and compression boots round it out. It sits in the Arts and Industrial Estate, where Byron does its best work. Come for the Pilates. Book the sauna while you are there.
