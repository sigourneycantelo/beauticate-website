Morgan was voted Brisbane's best pilates instructor by the Courier Mail in 2021, so Power Moves comes with a pedigree. The Newstead studio on Helen Street holds two spaces and the classes are the athletic kind. Reformer, hot mat, yoga, fifty minutes of proper work. There is a West End studio too, for the south-side crowd.
The fit-out is boutique and considered rather than clinical, which matters when you are face down on the carriage at 6am. This is pilates with intent, built for people who want to feel it the next day. Turn up. Push through. Book the earlier class than you think you can manage.
