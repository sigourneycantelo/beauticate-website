Anxious, foggy and not sleeping, with blood tests that come back fine? This week on Beautiful Inside I'm joined by Dr Fatima Khan, the Melbourne doctor behind the new book Hormone Health Is Mental Health. She says hormones can be the missing piece, and that perimenopause can show up in the brain well before the hot flushes arrive.

## In this episode you will learn

- Why the first signs of perimenopause often show up in the brain in your early 40s.
- How falling progesterone takes away the body's natural calm.
- Why it can be mistaken for other conditions, including ADHD.
- Why to track your symptoms for three months before you see your GP.
- How to protect your sleep, and what's in Fatima's Sacred Six.
