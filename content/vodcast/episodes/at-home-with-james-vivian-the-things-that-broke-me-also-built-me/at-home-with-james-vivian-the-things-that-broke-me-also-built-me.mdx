This week on Beautiful Inside I'm at home with James Vivian, one of Australia's most respected dermal clinicians, for a deeply personal conversation about identity, addiction, recovery and the long road to self-acceptance.

We talk about growing up feeling different, being bullied and coming out as a teenager, what led him to rehab and sobriety, and the family therapy that healed decades of resentment. Then we get to the sunscreen controversy, skincare myths, ADHD, and what's in his Sacred Six.

## In this episode you will learn

- Why the things that hurt James most also made him the clinician he is.
- How a lockdown business boom led to burnout, rehab and sobriety.
- What four sessions of family therapy changed for James and his parents.
- Why he follows up with every client, and what he says about the sunscreen debate.
- What's in James's Sacred Six.
